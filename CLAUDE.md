# Karins jigsaw – project guide

Karins jigsaw lets a person make a jigsaw puzzle from any picture and put it together on the screen, alone or with others: users, rounds (one game of a jigsaw by one user), timing, and a guide in nine languages. Everything saves by itself. Built on the same structure as Seven Friends.

What is decided but not done yet is in `TODO.md` (one chapter for each thing, e.g. "Future account settings"): read it before changing an area it names.

## Structure
- `frontend/` – Angular 20 (standalone components, signals, Bootstrap 5 + SCSS, Transloco), hosted on Firebase Hosting site `karins-jigsaw` (target `jigsaw`, web app `karins-jigsaw`). The app opens with Karins jigsaw.
- `functions/` – Node 22 + TypeScript Express backend, deployed as ONE Cloud Function, `jigsawApi`, in the codebase `jigsaw` (served at `/jigsaw/api/**`: the jigsaws, their pieces and pictures, the users and the plays; code in `functions/src/jigsaw/`, `createJigsawApp`). What the routes share is in `functions/src/common/` (the list of documents `jigsaw-list.ts`, the document store, the allowed origins). The frontend calls it through `environment.jigsawApi`; Hosting (`firebase.json`) and the dev proxy (`frontend/proxy.conf.json`) send `/jigsaw/api` to the function.
- `functions/src/models/shared/` – **the model, the ONE place to edit shared code.** `scripts/sync-models.mjs` copies it to `frontend/src/app/models/shared/` (generated, gitignored, never edit that copy). Shared files may only import from each other.
- `client-wrappers/` – the native apps that wrap the live site, copied from Seven Friends and renamed (app id `io.karinsjigsaw.app`): `android/` and `ios/` (Capacitor), `windows/` (.NET WPF + WebView2). **All wrappers always open the play module, `https://karins-jigsaw.web.app/play` (`server.url` in `frontend/capacitor.config.json`, `AppUrl` in `windows/MainWindow.xaml.cs`; change them together).** Read `client-wrappers/README.md` first: it lists what was taken out of the Seven Friends copy (alarms, sounds, push, tray, share target, and every permission except the internet, and since then only the camera is let in, where Take a photo needs it (iPhone: why it is used; Windows: asked, for the app's own pages; Android: none): keep it so). Icons: `npm run icons`. Scripts: `npm run android:sync`, `ios:sync`, `windows:run`, `windows:release`, `android:config`, `ios:config`.

### The model (`functions/src/models/shared/`)
A saved jigsaw is a `JigsawSpec` and is checked by `JigsawSettings`; the pieces on the board are placed objects (`PlacedFigure`) and `Puzzle` is what they do together.
| file | what is in it |
|---|---|
| `jigsaw-settings.ts` | a saved jigsaw as a document (`JigsawSpec`: name, size, grid, difficulty, line and colours, owner, visibility, timed) and `JigsawSettings`, which checks one |
| `jigsaw-kind.ts` | the kind of an entry (`JigsawKinds`): a ready jigsaw (what a document that says nothing is) or a **picture** (only the stored picture; the pieces and the difficulty are chosen in each round), and the size of a picture (`pictureSize`) |
| `jigsaw.ts` | the cut into pieces (`JigsawCut`), `JigsawPieces`, `pieceGrid`, limits |
| `cut-shape.ts` | the shapes of the tabs (`CutShapes`: classic, round, pointed, square, wave): each piece carries its own (`shape`), chosen at the start of a round of a picture (`PlayChoice.cutShape`) or for a ready jigsaw (`cutShape`); the pieces are squares on a grid whatever the shape |
| `play.ts` | a round (`Plays`, `PlaySpec`): positions, settings of the player, time played; a round of a picture also has its own cut, difficulty and pieces (`Plays.jigsawFor`, `startOwn`, `choiceError`) |
| `jigsaw-texts.ts` | the words of a picture in the languages (`JigsawTexts`): written in its base language, translated into the others by the service |
| `tile-shape.ts` | the shape of a picture tile of the home page and of a package (`TileShapes`): the picture's own, between 2:5 and 5:2 |
| `popularity.ts` | what makes a jigsaw popular (`Popularity`: rounds played and being new) |
| `jigsaw-user.ts` | the users who play (`JigsawUsers`) |
| `placed.ts`, `puzzle.ts`, `geometry.ts`, `base-object.ts` | placed objects (the pieces are placed objects), `Puzzle`: what they do together (snap, layers, groups, collecting pieces), points, boxes and the part of a picture a frame shows, the settings of an object (`BaseObject`) |

### The app and its routes
**The site has three parts (`app.routes.ts`; the root, `app.ts`, only holds the router outlet): the app, `/play` (`frontend/src/play/`), where everything a player does is; the admin, `/jigsaw` (`frontend/src/app/admin/`); and signing in, `/login` (`app/logons/`).** Home, `/`, and every address that is not known go to `/play/list`; `/install`, a short address that is easy to share, goes to `/play/info/install`. There is no website beside the app and no modes, and **there are on purpose no redirects for the old website addresses** (no players use them). (`/jigsaw/api/**`, the collections `jigsaw...`, the Storage paths, the function `jigsawApi` and the Hosting target are not pages and keep their names.)

**The app.** The route `play` loads `play/play.routes.ts`: **the main page is the main route and the pages of the app are its sub routes** (`/play` goes to the list):
- `/play/list`, the home page: the picture list (**its menu entry Preferences opens a panel over it with only the choices of that page, theme and the Home page ones, and a button to all preferences**, `play-home-panel`; with, on the top, the band of the rounds that are going, `app-my-rounds`, which the preferences have a switch for, `ListView.ongoing`, and a switch My favorites in the menu (**shown only while the user has favorites, or while the list is only the favorites**) that lists only the pictures the user has ticked with a heart, `ListView`).
- `/play/account` the account (who is on and all that has to do with the account, also who is signed in and Sign out; `pages/account/`) and `/play/account/preferences` the preferences.
- `/play/mine/addpicture` adding a picture (`pages/add-picture/`, which frames `play-picture-form`, made of shared components such as `app-jigsaw-header`, `app-category-picker` and `app-visibility-choice`, with its own class `NewPicture` for the saving and `CutChoice` for how a ready jigsaw is cut; **after Save one stays on the picture, to look over what the AI filled in**; **it takes a picture file or a photo taken there and then (Take a photo, on every platform: a phone or tablet opens its camera app, a computer shows the camera in a window, `app-camera-capture`; `cameraWay`), and makes a picture or a ready jigsaw (the same page, the kind chosen at the top: one size and one cut for both, which stay when the kind is changed: the picture gets a cut by itself and a small icon on it opens the cropper to adjust it, and a ready jigsaw also has pieces, difficulty and the clock)**; when the picture is saved it goes to the page of that picture).
- `/play/mine/many` many pictures at once (`pages/many/`, **for a publisher or an admin only: `publisherGuard`; the user menu has the entry Publish for them (`MenuMay.publish`), and these pages have `publishMenu`**; it frames `app-jigsaw-batch`). The entries Information (`/play/mine/info`) and Offers (`/play/mine/offers`) are for them too: `pages/feed/`, a list with a search and a status filter and a form, **the tiles of the home page that a publisher or an admin writes, their own (an admin any), with the words in every language that the AI makes when a tile is saved**, see `docs/guide/play-app.md`. So are Packages (`/play/mine/packages`, with the detail `/play/mine/packages/:id/pictures` where many pictures are uploaded; a package has a price or is free and holds ordinary pictures linked to it by `packageId`, not in the general list; its tile shows a random picture of it and opens `/play/package/:id`; **there the publisher's account name is shown (the pictures have no stamp there), and the person adds it to their pictures or buys it (nothing is charged yet), it is kept in `jigsaw-users/<id>/bought-packages`, and only then are its pictures in their search and theirs to open and play** (`BoughtPackages.mayUse`); `/play/packages` shows all packages). **Every tile is public, private or hidden: a private one is shown to its owner only, with a clear banner so it is not forgotten, and a new one is private to begin with**.
- `/play/mine/pictures` the pictures the user made (`app-my-jigsaws`; a row opens `/play/mine/picture/:id` (`pages/picture/`): **the form of a picture is one component, `play-picture-form` (`components/picture-form/`), framed into two pages, Add picture and the page of a picture, so the layout is the same each time**, and the page of a picture has **the user's own picture open for changes at once** (no Edit to press first; Save changes, shown only when something is changed, and Cancel or Close are in the form; Save stays on the page, Cancel or Close goes back to the list) with **Start playing on top to the left** (to `/play/round/:id`) and **a red Delete on top to the right**, and the rounds of the picture (`play-picture-rounds`): **only the owner (or an admin) changes it, and not its picture, size or cut once rounds have been played of it** (its owner can delete all the rounds to change them and cut the saved picture again; the AI fills what is empty of a new picture, or of one whose picture file was replaced, as it is saved); the helper's `pictureMenu` has only the way back) and `/play/mine/rounds` the rounds the user played (`app-my-round-list`).
- **`/play/mine` (no page of its own: it goes to the page of it that was open last, My pictures to begin with) and its sub pages are the user's own and show the user menu**: My pictures, My rounds, Add picture and, for a publisher or an admin, Publish (`publishMenu`: My stuff, Packages for the public, Many pictures, Information, Offers); the user menu ends with Help and the account (its alias). The pages outside it show the page menu: Packages, My stuff (to `/play/mine`), Filter (`/play/prefs`), **Preferences** (`/play/account/preferences`, for everybody) and, for nobody, "Logon" (`/play/account`); a user's account (its alias) is in the user menu only. ****Add picture** and, where the device can show a camera, **Take a photo** (`photo` in `menus.ts`, `PhotoShot`: it opens the camera at once, and the photo goes to Add picture) are in the user menu only, near its end (not in the menu of the home page), then **Help, which is last or, where the account follows it, second last** (the account is always the very last of a menu that has it). A menu is kept small, six entries or so besides the name of the app: what is chosen once is a preference** (the language and the rounds on the home page are there).
- `/play/jigsaw/:id` the puzzle of a jigsaw (`pages/jigsaw/`; **a round opens it, and the page before it** (`/play/round/:id`): the board `app-puzzel-board` and the details `app-puzzel-details` (with the time played of a timed round), both of the round `PuzzelRound`, which the page provides; the page has no menu of its own: **the page does not scroll: it is as big as the screen, the round and Look and sound are panels over the puzzle** (upper right corner, begin closed; on a small screen a click outside them closes them); the working area reaches the top of the screen, and the picture lies `areaMargin` (a twentieth of the shorter side of the picture area, at least 20 px) in from its top and left edge; the helper's `jigsawMenu` has switches (`toggle` on a menu entry, `PuzzelView`) for the help picture, the details, **Look** (Look and sound: a panel with this round's own settings, stored on the round) and the border pieces (and, for a user, **My stuff**, next to the logo; only the corner and edge pieces in the store; and **Full screen** (`play/full-screen.ts`; no switch on an iPhone or in the native apps: a browser fills the screen only on a press, so with the switch on it does so on the press that leads to a puzzle, and gives it back when the puzzle is left; Esc or the back gesture switches it off); **a puzzle that is finished has only Details and Table**: no help picture and no border pieces, `PuzzelView.solved`); a picture has no round yet: it goes to `/play/round/:id`).
- `/play/round/:id` **the page before the puzzle, where every card leads** (`pages/round/`, with Cancel back to where one came from): for a picture the number of pieces and the difficulty are chosen in the shared `app-round-choice`, the round is started and the puzzle opens with `?round=` (a round that is going is gone straight to); **a ready jigsaw is looked at first** (picture, facts, Start the round, Continue or Look); under it the picture is shared and the rounds played of it, also other players', are listed to look at.
- `/play/info` the info about the app (`pages/info/`, **reached by Help, the last entry of every menu**; a route with five pages, `/play/info/welcome`, `help`, `about`, `terms` and `install` (`/play/info` opens the one that was open last, the help to begin with), **one page component whose route data `doc` says which document it frames, and a menu of its own, `infoMenu`, with every page as an entry, after Back**, which returns to the page one came from however many info pages were opened (in the menu only, the pages have no button of their own; see `play-app.md`): the welcome (`app-welcome`, `jigsaw/welcome/`: Karin's welcome), the help, the about page and **the terms of use** (`app-guide-document`, `guide/guide-document.ts`; `jigsaw-terms.md` in each language: use at own risk, only legal pictures, what pictures are used for (Google Firebase, OpenAI), publishers selling packages and **the buyer getting the money back when a bought package or a picture in it is removed**; nothing is charged yet, so keep the text in step when payment is built, and its date) and how to install the app (`app-install-document`, `guide/install-document.ts`, with its own Share button: the Android and Windows downloads from `public/installs/` (offered only when the file is on the site), how to put the site on the home screen of an iPhone or iPad (Safari: Share, Add to Home Screen) and how to install it from Chrome or Edge; the visitor's device comes first (`core/platform.ts`: `platformOf`, `isInApp`; the Install entry is hidden when the app already runs as an app); its texts are the scope `install`)).
- `/play/prefs` the picture preferences (in the page menu, as Filter: `pages/prefs/`: a page made of the shared component `app-jigsaw-filter`, "Filter pictures"; what is ticked is kept on the device at once, `play.list`, **and with the user in the database (`listFilter` on the jigsaw user, a pause after the last choice; `JigsawUsers.change`), so it is the same on every device**, and is what the picture list is filtered by (`PlayListFilter` in `play-list-filter.ts`: the user's own is taken when the app opens or another account is chosen; **when a picture is saved its categories and who sees it (private, to begin with) are added to it**, `JigsawFilters.withCategories` and `withVisibility`, unless nothing is chosen in that choice, since the list shows everything then); **the filter also has Public and Private at the top (`visibilities` on `JigsawFilter` and `JigsawQuery`, a list like the others: one, both or none; none or both is every picture; they are always offered, whether or not there are private pictures)**.

**The frame of the app.** `PlayMain` (`play-main/`; **it is the one thing that scrolls**: it fills the screen and scrolls with the page that is open in it, since the window itself never does; the frame with the helper is fixed over it) has **one `<router-outlet>`** (the page that is open), the band of an admin who is in an account (`app-admin-banner`) and **the frame** (`play-frame/`, `.play-frame` in `styles.scss`: it lies over the page and everything on it, takes no clicks itself, and holds the helper). **The helper lives always on the main level** (`helper/helper.ts`, one `play-helper` in the frame: movable, kept inside the window, its place kept on the device, **the language is the last row of the home page menu before Help only for nobody (`app-language-select`); a user has it in the preferences**; **the last entry of every menu is Help** (or second last, before the account); **a section (Help, My stuff, Publish) opens where the person was last in it (`play/last-page.ts`)**; **when it opens or its words make it bigger it grows from the side nearest to the edge of the window (a menu in the right corner opens to the left, one at the bottom opens upwards) and keeps its room to that edge**; not shown while it has no menu). **Dragged to an edge of the window (top, bottom, left or right) and let go it sticks there as a bar** (`.play-bar`; the drag is `helper/menu-drag.ts`: **nothing on the page changes until the release**, see `play-app.md`): across the top or bottom the entries side by side, **each icon with its word under it**, down a side one under the other (icons only on a narrow screen); the language is a button whose list opens over the page, and **that it can be moved is shown without words** (a grip on the menu, which calls for attention until it has been moved once, and lit edges while it is carried; no hint text). **There is no folding or collapsing**: a menu always shows its entries. **The page makes room for the bar** (`--app-play-bar-top`, `-bottom`, `-left`, `-right`, set by the helper; the puzzle measures its room again). Dragged away from the edge it is the floating menu again. **What it shows is a menu**: a list of entries, one under the other, each an icon, the words and the page a press goes to (`helper/menus.ts`). **The first entry of every menu is the name of the app** (the logo, always home, `/`; **`app-home`, `app/home/home.ts`: the logo and always "Karins jigsaw", all of it a link to home, the one every place with the logo uses** (the menu, floating and as a bar, the welcome, the admin, signing in)), so it is on every page, inside the helper. A press is plain routing (`routerLink`): **there are no helper views and no helper subclasses**. **Main finds out whether a user is logged on** (a user is logged on when an account plays: `JigsawUserService.acting`) as it is shown (`ngOnInit`: the earlier login is restored, the account that played last is read), **and gives the helper the menu of the open page for that answer**. **Every route carries a page and the menu that goes with it** (route `data.menu`: a function of who plays, in `helper/menus.ts`; `play-route.ts` reads it), and the helper shows that menu inside its button; a menu may be none for a case (`/play/account` and `/play/account/preferences` have `accountMenu`: the way home, Account, Preferences, (Admin, which goes to `/jigsaw`: for an admin and, while there is no admin, for every account that plays, `JigsawUserService.mayOpenLevels`) and Help for a user, the way home and Help for nobody). **The background is the account's theme** (`PlayLook`: **Spring to begin with**; also Jigsaw, the soft pattern and the colours of the app, other tables, seasons and moods), painted on the body behind the pages and the menu; **the theme also gives every other background (bands, menu header) and the colouring and effects of the text** (`styles.scss`, at the tables; a new place with a background goes in `$themed-places`). **The text has one basic size and one basic family** that everything follows (`--app-basic-font-size`, `--app-font-sans`), and both are the account's choice (`TextLooks`, `look-and-texts.md`). **With the Jigsaw theme the soft pattern is behind the pages (`play-main`; not behind the puzzle) and the same wash is in the header of the helper's menu.** **Rules:** the pages are the app's own (`play/pages/`), made of the shared components and services of `app/`; what two pages need is a component, framed by both. Its texts are the scope `play` (`public/i18n/<language>/play.json`), in all nine languages like the rest (`npm run check:i18n`); until a language is chosen the language of the browser is used when the app has it.

**The admin, `/jigsaw`** (`app/admin/`, loaded when it is first opened, `loadChildren`; nothing of playing is here): `/jigsaw` is the levels of the accounts (`account-levels/`, `app-account-levels`: an admin also goes into an account there, which opens `/play/account` as that account, and changes or deletes an account) and `/jigsaw/ai-use` what the AI has used for the account (`ai-use/`, `ai-usage.service.ts`). `admin.routes.ts`; the frame `admin-shell.ts` (one bar: the logo home, the two pages, who is logged on, the language, Back to the app; the band `app-admin-banner` under it, whose way back goes to `/jigsaw`); `admin.guard.ts`: `adminGuard` (`mayOpenLevels`) and `adminOnlyGuard` (an admin, the AI use). A visitor who is not logged on goes to `/play/account?next=...` and back; an account that may not goes home.

**Signing in** (`app/logons/logons.routes.ts`): `/login`, `/login/email` and `/login/link` at the top level, each framed by `logon-shell.ts` (the logo, the way back to the app, and the language). Somebody signed in is sent on from them to `/play/account` (`guestGuard`), which says who is signed in and has Sign out.

- `board/` – the board the pieces are played on: `jigsaw-board/` (`JigsawBoard`: the picture area, the store of pieces and all pointer tools), `board-shape/` (`BoardShape`: draws a shape as SVG) and `drag.ts`.
- `logons/` – signing in with Firebase (the pages at `/login`, the services, and what the account page frames about logins).
- `guide/` – the documents the info pages frame (`guide-document.ts`, `install-document.ts`). `core/` – `api-collection.ts` (the base of the API services), `local-path.ts` (`isLocalPath`: an address a login may come back to), `api-error.ts` (`apiError`: why the API said no), `stored-user.ts` (where the id of the account that plays is kept), `preferences.ts` (`preferenceSignal`: a choice of a page, kept by itself), `one-pointer.ts` (`takenByAnother`: **what is dragged follows one finger only**; the board, the helper picture and the picture cropper use it, and a new drag does too), `session-storage.ts` (choices kept for the session), `theme.ts` (`cssVar`, `cssNumber`, `isWideScreen`: code that draws on a `<canvas>` element reads colours and sizes from the look), `i18n.ts`, `language.service.ts`, `user-header.interceptor.ts` and the Transloco files.
- `src/styles.scss` – **the one place for the look**: fonts, colours, sizes, layers and every custom class, as `--app-*` tokens on `:root` (Bootstrap's own variables are set from them) and responsive rules (`from`, `below`, `touch` mixins; breakpoints `$bp-*`). Components use Bootstrap classes and the classes/tokens of this file: no hard-coded colour, font or size in a template, an SVG attribute, drawing code or a component `styles:`. Code that draws on a `<canvas>` element reads tokens with `cssVar`/`cssNumber`.
- `models/shared/` – the model (generated copy, never edited).
- Backend: `common/http.ts` (every router of the API is made with `apiRouter`, so an `async` handler that fails is answered 503, never left hanging; Express 4 does not catch it by itself; also `bad` for an answer that says why not and `askerOf` for who asks), `common/ttl-cache.ts` (`TtlCache`: an answer kept for some seconds), `common/openai-chat.ts` (`askOpenAi`, what the classification and the translation share), `common/filter-async.ts`, `common/jigsaw-list.ts` (the routes of the list of jigsaw documents: they only check, call `stored()` and the store), `common/document-store.ts` (the Firestore code: list, add, replace, delete).

## Rules for the code
1. **Classes work on their own object.** A rule belongs on the class that owns the data, at the right level of the parent/child hierarchy: what every object has is on `BaseObject`, where a piece is and what its place means on `PlacedFigure`, what the pieces do together on `Puzzle`. A new kind of object overrides small hooks instead of adding `if (kind === …)` branches. Placed objects know their own geometry; the puzzle does what they do together.
2. **Model objects never change.** Moving or any other change gives a new object (or a new puzzle, or the same one when nothing changed). Components keep the plain stored data in a signal and make the classes from it when they need them (`Puzzle.from(...)`).
3. **Components are thin.** A component turns what the person does into calls on the model classes and shows the result. Business rules do not live in components or in the routes.
4. **What is stored is decided by the model.** `X.stored(...)` says which fields are saved (only the known ones); `X.fromStored(...)` reads a document that an older version saved and gives it what it lacked. Routes only call them. Never save a field that is not known.
5. **One validation, used everywhere.** `error()` on the classes (`BaseObject.of(data).error()`, `JigsawSettings(...).error()`) is used by the forms, the page and the API, so they cannot disagree.
6. Keep files small and about one thing; no unused code (check with `tsc --noUnusedLocals`); comments say *why* in plain words.
7. Always use Bootstrap in pages; custom CSS only if absolutely necessary, and then in `styles.scss` (inline styles only where a value is computed, such as a position or size from the data).
8. Never edit anything in a `lib/` or `dist/` directory (compiled output): edit the `.ts` source and rebuild.
9. Do not guess when debugging: establish the mechanism (exact line/condition) before fixing; if it is not found, ask for evidence.
10. **Stored pixels, drawn at a scale.** Sizes and positions in the data are stored pixels and never depend on the screen. The board draws the pieces area at `scale` (`jigsaw-board.ts`: the picture and the space round it fitted to the room, whole and in proportion, at most 1, at least 0.1), and every pointer position is turned back with `/ scale()` before it reaches the model. The store frame is beside the picture, or below it on a screen narrower than `--app-store-below` (1200 px, `stacked`); this is only how it is drawn: the store is then a short strip (`STACKED_STORE_ROWS` grid steps high) that scrolls sideways only, the tall store cut into bands side by side; `place()` and `areaPoint()` map between a stored place and its place in the strip. Beside the picture the frame scrolls up and down only: a store wider than the frame is shown in slices one under the other (`sliceCount`), the same kind of mapping. The stored coordinates of the pieces never change. New pointer code must do the same; the handles divide their size by `--app-scale` so they stay finger-sized.
11. **Small screens.** Below 1200 px (`--app-sidebar-fold`, `--app-store-below`, `$bp-xl`) the store frame of a puzzle is below the picture (rule 10) and a click outside the panels over the puzzle closes them (`isWideScreen`). The app's menu may be stuck to an edge as a bar (icons only down a side of a narrow screen); the page makes room for it, and a page that fills the screen leaves room for a bar at the bottom with `footerTop()` of `core/theme.ts`.
12. **Every page remembers what the person last chose.** A choice of the person's own on a page (a view, a tab, a switch, a selection, a filter, where a floating frame was put, the defaults of a new picture) is kept on the device in `localStorage` with `preferenceSignal` (a signal that keeps itself) or `readPreference`/`writePreference` (`core/preferences.ts`; the value is checked when it is read, storage that is missing or refuses means the page starts with its default) and used when the page is opened again, also another day. Kept: My pictures (`jigsaw.userVisibility`, `jigsaw.userSort`, `jigsaw.userJigsawText`, rows or cards `jigsaw.userView`, what a card shows `jigsaw.userCardInfo`), the same two of My rounds (`jigsaw.roundsView`, `jigsaw.roundsCardInfo`; the switches are `app-view-switch`), the helper of a round (`jigsaw.helper`, `jigsaw.edgesOnly`, `jigsaw.helperFrame`, `jigsaw.helperScale`), that a piece has been moved once, so the store need not say how (`jigsaw.hasMoved`), the table the pieces lie on, how the pieces look, the effects and the sound (`jigsaw.table`, `jigsaw.pieces`, `jigsaw.effects`, `jigsaw.sound`, `jigsaw.soundStyle`, `PlayLook`) and whether the effects are open (`jigsaw.effectsOpen`), the size and font of the text (`jigsaw.textLook`, `TextLook`), the size of the picture tiles (`jigsaw.tileSize`, `PartSizeChoice`; a new grid of tiles in the app takes the class `tile-grid`; **the home page, the pages of a package (also the upload of its pictures) and the cards of My pictures are not columns but `tile-rows`: tiles in the shapes of their pictures, lines filled from side to side, the size saying how high a line is**; a small label is a Bootstrap `.badge`, which has one size), whether pictures have their effects (`jigsaw.pictureEffects`, `PictureEffects`, also with the account), how much the picture tiles tell (`jigsaw.tileDetails`, `TileDetails`: none, only the picture; short, the name and for a ready jigsaw pieces and difficulty; the owner's account alias is stamped on the picture in every grade; details, also the categories, the date and the marks; full, also the picture's description. A detail on a tile has the class `tile-details`, and all its words are in `tile-text`), the filter and the switches of the list (`play.list`, `play.listOngoing`, `play.listFavorites`), where the menu of the app was put and that it has been moved (`play.helperSpot`, `play.helperMoved`; its place by name, left, right, top, bottom or floating, is also with the account, `menuPlace`, `MenuSpot`, and is chosen in the preferences, card Appearance, under Menu; the switch of the rounds on the home page is in the same card, under Home page, `showOngoing`), the page that was open last in each section (`play.last.info`, `play.last.mine`, `play.last.publish`), whether a puzzle fills the screen (`play.fullScreen`, `FullScreen`), what is typed to find an account (`play.userFilter`), what was chosen for the last new picture (`play.newPicture`: picture or ready jigsaw, size, number of pieces, difficulty, timed, shape; not the name, picture or description, which belong to that picture) and whether its Advanced part is open (`play.addPictureAdvanced`), the choices of many at once (`jigsaw.batch`, the language too), what was chosen for the last round (`jigsaw.roundDefaults`, kept as each choice is made), what is typed and the status chosen in the lists of Information, Offers and Packages (`play.feedText.<kind>`, `play.feedStatus.<kind>`), whether the explanation on the list of packages is shown (`play.packagesHelp`), the language, free or not and currency of the last new tile (`play.newTile`) and whether its Advanced part is open (`play.feedAdvanced`), the language (`jigsaw.language`), and who plays (`jigsaw.userId`). Not kept, on purpose: what covers the page (the panels over the puzzle), the Cheat switch, and what is typed for one picture (what is kept for the session only is in `sessionStorage`, `core/session-storage.ts`, such as the page one was on before the info). A new choice on a page is kept in the same change that adds it.

13. **Layers (z-index) are in bands, set in `styles.scss` as `--app-z-*` tokens: no other number in a template or a component.** `0 – 99` the page and its content (Bootstrap's `z-1` … `z-3` lift one thing over its neighbours); `100 – 199` what is round the page (the sticky bands `--app-z-band`, the bars `--app-z-bar`); `200 – 899` **the play**: the working area of a round (the yellow area, `.board-root`, `--app-z-play`) and all that is in it, which is a stacking context of its own and only orders itself inside (`--app-z-pieces`, `--app-z-controls`); `900` and up **the overlays, always over the play**: menus and popups that open (`--app-z-overlay`, Bootstrap's dropdown is 1000), the panels over the puzzle (`--app-z-sidebar`), the helper picture (`--app-z-helper`), the dialogs (`--app-z-dialog`; Bootstrap's modal, popover, tooltip and toast are above 1050). A header that has a menu open is lifted over the play (`.app-header:has(.dropdown-menu.show)`), since the menu is part of the bar. A new popup or overlay takes a token from the top band; a new part of the play takes none (it lives inside the play).

14. **A bar stays on ONE line, and the words in it are shown only while everything fits on one line.** The bar of the admin carries `appLabelFit` (`core/label-fit.ts`): it measures, not guessing from the screen width. If the bar wraps, the words of its buttons are hidden (`fit-1`: the icons stay, and the words stay for a screen reader; `.fit-label` in `styles.scss`). The name beside the logo always stays. A new bar that must stay on one line takes the directive and puts `fit-label` on the words that may go. (The app's menu as a bar does not use it: across the top or bottom each icon has its word under it.) Everything written in the bar of a frame (`.app-header`, the admin and signing in) has one text size, from the common class `menu-style` (in `styles.scss`: the normal text size for the links, the switches, the name and the buttons): put it on the bar, not a size on each text. (The menu of the app has its own, `menu-font`.)

15. **All AI is done by the service (the API), and no AI key ever reaches a client.** The pages and the native apps only ask our API (`/jigsaw/api/**`); they never call an AI provider, and no key, token or setting of one is in their code, their build or an answer of the API. A new AI feature is a route of the API. The key is in `/.env` and, deployed, in the Firebase secret; `functions/.secret.local` (the emulator's copy) is left out of a deploy by `ignore` of the functions in `firebase.json` (the Firebase CLI would pack it otherwise: keep it there). Test: `functions/test/ai-key.test.ts`.

## Shared Firebase project (IMPORTANT)
The Firebase project `sevenfriends` is shared with Seven Friends. Therefore:
- Deploy only `hosting:jigsaw` and `functions:jigsaw` (the functions codebase; the npm `deploy*` scripts do; `npm run deploy:jigsaw` is the site alone). Never `firebase deploy` everything, and never deploy `firestore`/`storage` rules or indexes from here: that would overwrite Seven Friends' live ones. `firestore.rules` and `storage.rules` here are for the emulator only. Change live rules/indexes from the Seven Friends repo. What the jigsaw needs is written in this repo's `firestore.indexes.json`, `firestore.rules` and `storage.rules`; `npm run sync:firestore` (`scripts/sync-firestore.mjs`) READS the live indexes and rules from Firebase, MERGES in what the jigsaw lacks (it only adds, never changes Seven Friends' entries; rules go in a marked `BEGIN jigsaw` block before the live catch-all deny) and with `--apply` DEPLOYS the result straight to the project, touching no repo. No option shows the difference, `--check` exits 1 when live lacks something. Indexes need the logged-in firebase CLI; rules need a service account (`GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS` or `--key <file>`). This is the one allowed way to change live rules/indexes from here, and only with `--apply` when asked; a later deploy from the Seven Friends repo can replace the jigsaw's block, so run it again after that. A new query that needs a composite index is added to this repo's `firestore.indexes.json` first.
- The functions codebase is `jigsaw`, not `default`, so a deploy here never deletes Seven Friends' functions.
- Collections (`jigsaw`, `jigsaw-users`, `jigsaw-favorites`, `jigsaw-config`, `jigsaw-shares`) and Storage paths (`jigsaw/...`) are the jigsaw's own, so they cannot collide with Seven Friends' data. **The one exception is `user-account`**, Seven Friends' collection of accounts, which the sign-in with Firebase (`logons/`, `docs/guide/users-and-logon.md`) writes to the way Seven Friends' own `POST /users/sync` does (the same fields, only what a login proves) and never anything else of it.
- Do not run any `firebase` command that changes the shared project, and do not deploy, commit or push, unless asked.

## Running and testing
- `npm run install:all`, then `npm run dev` (functions watch + emulators + frontend). The frontend is on **http://localhost:4210**; emulator ports are offset from Seven Friends (UI 4010, Functions 5011, Firestore 8090, Auth 9109, Storage 9209, Hosting 5010) so both can run together.
- `npm test` runs the backend tests (Vitest). `npm run test:emulator` starts a Firestore emulator (test ports, not the dev ones) and runs the real stores against it (`functions/test-emulator/`). **The test emulators and the dev emulators each have a temporary folder of their own** (`scripts/with-java.mjs --temp=…`): the Storage emulator deletes its folder when it stops, and in the folder the computer shares that took away the dev pictures; keep it so for any new emulator script. The model has the real tests: every rule above is tested on the classes (`functions/test/`), the Firestore code with a small fake collection. A rule is not done until it has a test.
- `npm run build` builds both. The frontend build also copies the model (`sync:models`).
- **Version of the web app** (`major.minor.build`, e.g. 1.0.12): `scripts/sync-version.mjs` keeps the build number in `frontend/build-number.json` (committed; change major or minor there by hand) and **adds one at every frontend build** (`prebuild`: `npm run build`, `android:sync`, every deploy that builds); a dev server or test only writes the generated, gitignored `src/environments/version.ts` (marked dev) and `public/version.json`. The version is shown at **Help > About**. `core/app-update.ts` (`AppUpdate`, started in `app.ts`) asks for `/version.json` (Hosting sends it with no-cache) when the app opens, when the person comes back to it and every 10 minutes, and loads the web app again when the live version is newer (at once at start and on return; while working, when the person opens another page; once per version per tab, so it cannot loop; never on a dev server). The Android and Windows apps show the live site, so they follow it; their own versions (`build.gradle` `versionCode`/`versionName`, `KarinsJigsaw.csproj` and the installer `.iss`) are separate and only change when a new APK or installer is made.
- The frontend has no unit tests: check pages in the browser (the dev server must be the current one; restart it if the page shows old code).
- **Scroller membership check:** `scripts/board-scroller-membership.js` (run in the browser on a round) drops one piece at many points over and round the scroller and says if the page's answer to "is it in the scroller" (`inStore`) differs from what the screen shows (rule 3, whole inside; along the way the scroller scrolls only the store's own length counts); run it above and below 1200 px. Empty list is good.
- **Board reach check:** `scripts/board-reach-sweep.js` (run in the browser on a round, as a user who has one) takes a piece from the store and one from the picture to every side of the board and lists jumps, disappearances, pieces outside the board and room a piece cannot reach; run it in windows of several sizes (store below the picture under 1200 px, beside it from 1200 px; a tall picture also gives the free space beside the picture). `npm run test:reach` is the same idea as a Karma spec (`jigsaw-board.reach.spec.ts`, headless Edge/Chrome, `WINDOW=1400x800`) but is not reliable yet.


## What "the jigsaw" and "the picture area" mean
The program is called **Karins jigsaw** (the logo and the name of the app). In the rules, "the jigsaw" is the saved document (a ready jigsaw or a picture: the collection `jigsaw`, its API `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw`), and "the picture area" is the part of the board (`JigsawBoard`) the picture is on, where the pieces are put together. The page address `/jigsaw` is the admin, not a page of a jigsaw. **"The piece scroller area"** (also "the scroller") is the part of the board beside or below the picture area where the pieces wait and which scrolls (in the code the store, the store frame: `.store-scroller`).
