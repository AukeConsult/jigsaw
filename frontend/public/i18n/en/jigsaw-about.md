# About Karins jigsaw

Every picture has a story. Turn your own photos and memories into jigsaw puzzles: a summer by the sea, a birthday, a place you love, the faces of the people you care about. Then put them together piece by piece, and feel the moment come back to you.

Share your jigsaws with friends and family, and enjoy a puzzle together. It is always nicer with company.
