Turn any picture into a jigsaw and put it together on the screen. Everything is saved by itself, so you can stop and come back any time.

## Play

1. Press a picture on the home page. Choose the number of **Pieces** (from 20 to 1000), how easily a piece clicks in (**Snap**: **Easy**, **Medium** or **Hard**) and, if you like, **Timed**. Then press **Start the round**.
2. Drag the pieces from the frame beside the picture (below it on a small screen) to the picture.
3. Let go of a piece close to its place and it clicks in. Pieces that fit each other stick together.
4. The menu has switches: **Whole picture** shows the whole picture in a small frame, and **Edges only** shows only the edge pieces, so you can build the frame first. It switches itself off when every edge piece is in its place, and the rest are shown. **Round** shows the time played and has **Reset round** and **Delete round**, and **Look** chooses what the pieces lie on and the sound. **Full screen** lets the puzzle fill the whole screen, also the next time you open a puzzle (not on an iPhone or in the Android and Windows apps).

Scores will come later.

## Make your own

* Press **My stuff** in the menu and then **Add picture**, give it a name, choose a picture (or take a photo) and press **Save**. The name, and a description if you write one, are translated into the other languages when you save. Write them in the language the form says.
* A picture you add is **Private**: only you see it.
* To make pictures **Public**, so that everybody can play them, you must be approved as **Publisher**: send an e-mail to [karinauke2@gmail.com](mailto:karinauke2@gmail.com) and ask.
* You can also share a private picture with friends: press **Share** under it. Anybody who gets the link can play it.
* **My stuff** in the menu has **My pictures**, where you change or delete a picture you have made.

## Rounds

* One game is a round. **Continue** goes on with yours; **Look** shows the round of another player, which you cannot change.
* **My rounds**, under **My stuff**, has the rounds you have played. Press your name in the menu to see your account or to choose another.
