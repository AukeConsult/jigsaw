Turn any picture into a jigsaw and put it together on the screen. Everything is saved by itself, so you can stop and come back any time.

## Play

1. Press a picture on the front page. Choose the number of pieces and press **Start the round**.
2. Drag the pieces from the frame beside the picture (below it on a small screen) to the picture.
3. Let go of a piece close to its place and it clicks in. Pieces that fit each other stick together.
4. **Picture** shows the whole picture in a small frame, and **Edges** shows only the border pieces. The sidebar (**Details** on a small screen) has **Reset round** and **Delete round**.

## Make your own

Press **Add picture** in the menu, give it a name, choose a picture and press **Save**. Under **More options** you choose who sees it (**Public** / **Private** / **Hidden**).

## Rounds

* One game is a round. **Continue** goes on with yours; **Look** shows the round of another player, which you cannot change.
* Press your name in the menu (**Who plays?** if you have not chosen one) to see your rounds and pictures. There is also **Choose another account**.

## Info

**Info** in the menu has this help, **Share** and **Install the app**.
