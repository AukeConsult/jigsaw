Turn any picture into a jigsaw and put it together on the screen. Everything is saved by itself, so you can stop and come back any time.

## Play

1. Press a picture on the front page. Choose the number of **Pieces** (from 20 to 1000), how easily a piece clicks in (**Snap**: **Easy**, **Medium** or **Hard**) and, if you like, **Timed**. Then press **Start the round**.
2. Drag the pieces from the frame beside the picture (below it on a small screen) to the picture.
3. Let go of a piece close to its place and it clicks in. Pieces that fit each other stick together.
4. **Picture** shows the whole picture in a small frame, and **Edges** shows only the edge pieces, so you can build the frame first. The sidebar (**Details** on a small screen) has **Reset round** and **Delete round**.

Scores will come later.

## Make your own

* Press **Add picture** in the menu, give it a name, choose a picture and press **Save**.
* A picture you add is **Private**: only you see it.
* To make pictures **Public**, so that everybody can play them, you must be approved as **Publisher**: send an e-mail to [karinauke2@gmail.com](mailto:karinauke2@gmail.com) and ask.
* You can also share a private picture with friends: anybody who gets the link can play it.

## Rounds

* One game is a round. **Continue** goes on with yours; **Look** shows the round of another player, which you cannot change.
* Press your name in the menu (**Who plays?** if you have not chosen one) to see your rounds and pictures. There is also **Choose another account**.
