# Where a jigsaw is kept, and its files

Part of the project guide (moved out of `CLAUDE.md`; text unchanged). Read this before changing this area.

- Stored in the Firestore collection `jigsaw` through `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw` (same checks and shape as `jigsaw-list`; `JIGSAW` in the model, `JigsawService` in the frontend).
- **Where a jigsaw is kept:** its settings and its non-piece objects are the Firestore document `jigsaw/<id>` (collection `jigsaw`, `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw`). **Each piece is a document of its own** in the subcollection `jigsaw/<id>/pieces/<piece id>` (`JIGSAW_PIECES`, `JigsawPiece` in the model, `functions/src/jigsaw-pieces.ts`) with its `number` (the grid point it belongs on: row × columns + column + 1), `col`, `row`, its four edges, `size`, where it is (`centerX`, `centerY`), its `layer` and **`matched`** (it lies on its own place: the server works this out from where the piece is, `JigsawPieces.matched`, whatever the page says). The page does not put the pieces in the jigsaw document (`placed` there holds only what is not a piece). The background picture **is a file in Firebase Storage**, `jigsaw/<id>/picture`. (There is no picture of a piece; jigsaws saved earlier still have files `jigsaw/<id>/pieces/<piece id>`, which go with the jigsaw.)
- **A picture entry keeps only its picture** (`jigsaw/<id>/picture`): no pieces under it. Its rounds keep their own pieces as documents under the round (`jigsaw/<id>/plays/<play id>/pieces/<piece id>`, written with the round and deleted with it by `recursiveDelete`): the board draws every piece from the one picture.
- **Files go through the API, never from the page to Storage** (the way Seven Friends keeps its files): the page sends bytes to the API, the Cloud Function writes them with `firebase-admin` (`getStorage().bucket()`; the Admin SDK ignores the storage rules, which let nobody in, so the rules are not touched), the type is found from the first bytes (never from what the page says) and a file is read back through the API. The routes: `PUT/GET/HEAD /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-image/:id` (the picture: the raw bytes as the body, jpeg, png or webp, **at most about 5 MB**, 404 when there is no such jigsaw) and `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw-pieces/:id` (`GET` the pieces; `PUT {pieces: [...]}` when the jigsaw is cut: all the pieces are replaced). Deleting a jigsaw takes away its pieces and its picture.
- **Saving the game and reinstating it:** a cut sends all the pieces; after that `PieceSync` (`jigsaw/piece-sync.ts`) saves only the pieces that were moved, a pause (0.8 s) after the last move, and the server sets `matched`. Opening a round reads its pieces back (`PuzzelRound`): each is where it was left, on its layer, with its edges and number, and the Details panel shows **Matched: x of y** ("solved!" when all are; no progress bar). A matched piece is drawn like every other piece, with the line of the jigsaw (no green outline; only the blink when it clicks into place is green). A jigsaw that an older version saved with its pieces in its own document has them moved to the subcollection when it is opened.
- **Background picture:** a jpeg, png or webp chosen in the form of a picture, shown behind the grid points and objects and covering the picture area (not once it is cut: see "Helper banner and hidden picture" in `board-layout.md`). Before it is saved the page **cuts it to the shape of the picture area** (width : height). Choosing a file opens `app-picture-cropper`: the picture lies behind a frame with the shape of the picture area and is dragged (and zoomed, slider or wheel, up to 4 times) to find the cut; "Use this cut" gives the part of the picture the frame shows (`PictureCrop` in the model: it never lets the frame show what is outside the picture), Cancel keeps the old picture. `fitPicture` (`jigsaw/fit-picture.ts`) makes a jpeg of that part, up to twice the size of the picture area and at most about 5 MB (`MAX_PICTURE_BYTES`). What is saved, what the pieces show and what the helper picture shows is that cut picture, so the helper picture has the shape of the picture area. A saved picture that gets another size or number of pieces is read again and cut anew in the form (`NewPicture.useSaved`; only while nobody has played it). A new jigsaw is saved when Save is pressed: the document first, then its picture, since the picture belongs to its id.
- **The name of the picture file is kept with the jigsaw** (`JigsawSpec.pictureName`, at most `JIGSAW_MAX_PICTURE_NAME` = 200 characters): the form of a picture sends the name of the chosen file and does not show it; a change sends the name back (`NewPicture`; a save replaces the whole document).

**The pictures of the accounts** are files in the same bucket under `jigsaw/accounts/<id>/picture` (see `docs/guide/users-and-logon.md`), written and read by the backend only, like the pictures of the jigsaws.
