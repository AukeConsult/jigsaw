# Karins jigsaw

Make a jigsaw puzzle from any picture, and put it together on the screen. Everything is saved by itself, so you can stop and come back to the same game any time.

## Make a jigsaw

1. On the front page press **Make a jigsaw** (or open **Jigsaws** in the menu and press **New jigsaw**).
2. Type a **Name**. The **Author** is optional.
3. Choose a **Size** (the shape of the puzzle) and the number of **Pieces** (up to 1000; the numbers offered fit the shape of the size in whole rows and columns, and the size is adjusted a little so the pieces fit exactly). Press **Custom** under Size if you want to type your own width and height.
4. Choose a **Picture**. A window opens: drag the picture to find the part you want, zoom with the slider or the mouse wheel, then press **Use this cut**. The form then shows an example of the jigsaw: the picture put together from its pieces on the back colour, with the line and colours you chose and a few pieces lifted out, so you can see it before you save.
5. Press **Save and start**.

The puzzle is cut into whole pieces, so the size and the number of pieces can come out a little different from what you chose. The form tells you what you get.

## Play

* The pieces wait in a frame next to the picture (below it on a small screen). Drag a piece to the picture to build it. You can put a piece anywhere on the screen, and it stays where you leave it.
* When you let go of a piece close to its right place, it **clicks into place**: it blinks and your device vibrates. Pieces that fit each other **stick together**, and you move them as one. Hold **Shift** while you take a piece to pull it loose.
* Drag a frame on the empty space to pick several pieces and move them together.
* The sidebar shows how many pieces are in place (**Matched**). Placed pieces get a green outline.
* **Start over** shuffles all the pieces again. You are asked to approve first, because the progress is lost.
* **Difficulty** (Hard, Medium or Easy) is how close a piece must be to its place to click in: the easier, the farther away it can be. Medium is the usual.
* **Line** and **Line colour** set how thick (None, Hairline, Thin, Medium or Thick; the line looks the same on a big window and a small one) and what colour the line is, round each piece and round the whole picture area. **Back colour** is the colour of the picture area inside the frame. You can change all of them while you play.
* **Cheat (help find pieces)** is a switch that shows the number of each place on the picture.

## Help for building

These are in the menu at the top while you play. On a narrow screen these move to a line of their own under the menu.

* **Picture** (helper picture): shows the whole picture in a small frame on top of the page. Drag it anywhere on the frame to move it and its corner to make it bigger or smaller.
* **Edges** (only corner and edge pieces): hides the inside pieces, so you can build the border first.
* **Collect pieces:** click on the empty part of the store frame, and the pieces from the store are put together at the top of it so you can find them.

## Jigsaws

The list shows all your jigsaws with a small picture and the date they were made. Switch between **List** and **Cards** at the top. **Play** opens a jigsaw and **Delete** removes it for good (with its pieces and picture).

To change the name of a jigsaw, open it and click the title at the top of the sidebar.

## Small screens

On a phone or a small window the sidebar is folded away: press **Details** in the menu at the top (to the right of **Edges**) to open it, and click anywhere else, also on the empty parts of it, to close it again. The whole picture always fits on the screen. The pieces wait in a strip below it, which you scroll sideways.
