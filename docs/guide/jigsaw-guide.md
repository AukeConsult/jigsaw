# Karins jigsaw

Make a jigsaw puzzle from any picture, and put it together on the screen. Everything is saved by itself, so you can stop and come back where you left off any time.

## Who plays

The first time you open a jigsaw, or make one, you are asked who plays. The page shows the users that exist: press your name to play as you. If you are new, press **Make a user**: choose a **user name** (3 to 30 letters a-z, digits, _ or -; nobody else can have the same) and give your **name**, and if you like an **e-mail** address (optional; nobody sees it, it is only kept so that you can connect the user to a registration later), then press **Make the user and play**. There is no password for now, so anyone can pick any user from the list. Your name is shown in the menu at the top: press it to see who plays. There you can press **Edit** to change your user name, your name or your e-mail address (the address is never shown, not even to you; type a new one, or tick the box to take it away), or **Choose another user** to play as someone else. Your rounds stay yours when you change your user name. Under it you see **Your rounds**: all the rounds you have started, of every jigsaw, newest first, with how far each has come, a **Continue** (or **Look**) button and a **Delete** button (you are asked first).

## Make a jigsaw

1. On the front page press **Make a jigsaw** (or open **Jigsaws** in the menu and press **New jigsaw**).
2. Type a **Name**, and choose the **Author** from the users: it starts as you, and the author owns the jigsaw.
3. Choose a **Size** (the shape of the puzzle) and the number of **Pieces** (up to 1000; the numbers offered fit the shape of the size in whole rows and columns, and the size is adjusted a little so the pieces fit exactly). Press **Custom** under Size if you want to type your own width and height.
4. Choose a **Picture**. A window opens: drag the picture to find the part you want, zoom with the slider or the mouse wheel, then press **Use this cut**. The form then shows an example of the jigsaw: the picture put together from its pieces on the back colour, with the line and colours you chose and a few pieces lifted out, so you can see it before you save.
5. Press **Save and start**. The jigsaw is made and your first round of it starts.

The puzzle is cut into whole pieces, so the size and the number of pieces can come out a little different from what you chose. The form tells you what you get.

## Play

* The pieces wait in a frame next to the picture (below it on a small screen). Drag a piece to the picture to build it. You can put a piece anywhere on the screen, and it stays where you leave it.
* When you let go of a piece close to its right place, it **clicks into place**: it blinks and your device vibrates. Pieces that fit each other **stick together**, and you move them as one. Hold **Shift** while you take a piece to pull it loose.
* Drag a frame on the empty space to pick several pieces and move them together.
* The sidebar shows how many pieces are in place (**Matched**). Placed pieces get a green outline.
* **New round** starts another round of the same jigsaw, with the pieces scattered again. You are asked to approve first. The round you leave stays in your list of rounds, so nothing is lost. **Delete round** removes the round you are playing for good (you are asked first); the jigsaw and your other rounds stay.
* The **difficulty** (Hard, Medium or Easy) is how close a piece must be to its place to click in: the easier, the farther away it can be. It is a setting of the jigsaw, chosen when it is made, and the same in every round: the sidebar tells it but you cannot change it there.
* **Line** and **Line colour** set how thick (None, Hairline, Thin, Medium or Thick; the line looks the same on a big window and a small one) and what colour the line is, round each piece and round the whole picture area. **Back colour** is the colour of the picture area inside the frame. You can change all of them while you play.
* **Cheat (help find pieces)** is a switch that shows the number of each place on the picture.

## Help for building

These are in the menu at the top while you play. On a narrow screen these move to a line of their own under the menu.

* **Picture** (helper picture): shows the whole picture in a small frame on top of the page. Drag it anywhere on the frame to move it and its corner to make it bigger or smaller.
* **Edges** (only corner and edge pieces): hides the inside pieces, so you can build the border first.
* **Collect pieces:** click on the empty part of the store frame, and the pieces from the store are put together at the top of it so you can find them.

## Jigsaws

The list shows all your jigsaws with a small picture and the date they were made. Switch between **List** and **Cards** at the top. **Play** opens the jigsaw page and **Delete** removes it for good (with its pieces and picture).


## Rounds

Every jigsaw can be played many times, by many people. The page of a jigsaw lists the rounds of **everybody**, and yours are marked **Yours** and shown first. For each round you see who plays it, how many pieces are in place, and when it started. Press **Continue** to go on with one of yours (**Look** for a solved one), **New round** to start another, or **Delete** to remove one of yours for good (you are asked first). You can press **Look** on another player's round to see how far they have come, but you can never change or delete it: the pieces cannot be moved, the settings and the buttons that change a round are not shown, and you can start your own round from there. The picture, the size and the pieces are the jigsaw's and are the same in every round. The difficulty is part of the jigsaw too: it is chosen when the jigsaw is made (or edited) and every round of it is played with it, so you cannot change it in a round. The line and the colours are your own choice in each round, and do not change the jigsaw for anyone else. Press **Edit** on the page of a jigsaw. While nobody has started a round of it, everything can be changed: you get the form of a new jigsaw filled in, with the picture too (a new picture, another cut, another size or number of pieces). Once somebody has started a round, only the name, the author, the difficulty (every round then follows it) and the look that a new round starts with can be changed; the picture, size and pieces are fixed because every round shares them. Delete the rounds (if they are only yours) to be able to edit it all again.

## Small screens

On a phone or a small window the sidebar is folded away: press **Details** in the menu at the top (to the right of **Edges**) to open it, and click anywhere else, also on the empty parts of it, to close it again. The whole picture always fits on the screen. The pieces wait in a strip below it, which you scroll sideways.
