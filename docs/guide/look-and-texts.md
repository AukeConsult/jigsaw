# The look and the texts

Part of the project guide (moved out of `CLAUDE.md`; text unchanged). Read this before changing this area.

- **Font sizes: ONE basic size, and every other size is it times a factor** (`styles.scss`: `$font-full`, the size at Normal on a full-size screen, 18 px, 1400 px wide or more; a smaller view gets less, in a straight line down to `$font-on-phone` of it, 16 px, on a phone 375 px wide or less; the token is `--app-font-size`, and the whole scale is tuned there). The tokens: `--app-fs-title` ×1.6, `-heading` ×1.25, `-text` ×1, `-small` ×0.85, `-tiny` ×0.7, `-stamp` ×0.5 (the class `.stamp`: who a picture is from, written on the picture; the smallest of all and only for that, 8 to 9 px at the normal size; **always slim**, weight 300 of the standard family whatever family is chosen); and `-menu` ×1, `-compact` (the same as small: the name on a card), `-hint` ×0.65 (the word under an icon of a docked menu). Nothing else sets a text size: Bootstrap's `h1` and `.h1`, `h2`-`h6` and `.h2`-`.h6`, `.lead`, `.small`, `.btn`, `.form-label`, `.form-check-label`, `.form-text`, the fields, `.badge`, `.invalid-feedback` and the navbar name are set to the tokens there, and templates use `.page-title` (h1), `.section-title` (h2), `.subsection-title`, `.tiny` (dates, details), `.icon-md` instead of picking a Bootstrap heading size (`h3`, `h4`, `fs-3` ...). **Checked by measuring: every piece of text on every page is one of the sizes of the scale and the font is the system font (`--app-font-sans`; `--app-font-mono` for codes); the little house on the logo and the plus on an avatar are `--app-fs-tiny`.** Only two things are sized in pixels of their own, because they are drawn and not written: the numbers on the pieces and on the grid of the board. **A big icon over a message (nothing here, no match) is `.icon-xl`** (from the title size, so it follows the screen), never Bootstrap's `display-*` or `fs-*` (they are fixed sizes); a line of text takes `.subsection-title`, `.small` or `.tiny`, never `fs-5` or `fs-6`; **what is round the text is in `em` of a size of the scale** (the logo of the helper, `.play-helper .brand-lockup`, so it keeps its shape and follows the screen). No component sets a `font-size` of its own (the logo is on the scale too); a new size is not added: use the nearest. What each is for and how big it is at the normal text size (phone / wide screen, px, rounded): **title** (the page title) 26 / 29; **heading** (h2 and under, the line under a title, the name in the header, an icon beside a heading) 20 / 23; **text** (the text, what is typed in a field, the main button; at least 16 px in a field so a phone does not zoom in) 16 / 18; **small** (the label of a field and of a switch, the help and the error under a field, notes, the text of every other button) 14 / 15; **tiny** (dates, details, badges, the subtitle of the logo) 11 / 13.
- **One basic size and one basic family.** Every size of text is in `rem` or `em`, so all of it follows `--app-basic-font-size` on `:root` (never a size of text in `px`; the numbers drawn on the pieces and the grid are part of the picture). Every family is `--app-font-sans`. A person of the app chooses both on the account preferences (`TextLooks`: small, normal, large, larger; standard, clear, serif, playful, all fonts of the device; **"clear" is also more open**: more room between letters (`--app-letter-spacing`), words (`--app-word-spacing`) and lines, since its letters alone are close to the standard ones and on many devices the same; a rule with a letter spacing of its own adds `--app-letter-spacing` to it), kept on the device (`jigsaw.textLook`) and with the account (`textLook`), said by `play-main` (`data-text-size`, `data-font`). The admin and the pages of signing in keep the normal size and standard letters. On a touch screen a field is never under 16 px, or a phone zooms in on it.
- **The menu of the app has a font class of its own, `.menu-font`** (`--app-fs-menu`, a factor of its own, 16 px on a phone to 18 px on a wide screen): the floating menu, the bars and the list of languages that drops from a bar. **The words under the icons of a bar across the top or the bottom have a type of their own, `.menu-label`** (the menu's size, `--app-fs-menu`; on a phone, narrower than `$bp-md`, the small `--app-fs-hint`). The bars of the admin and of signing in (`.app-header`) take `.menu-style` (the text size).
- **Buttons have three sizes, also a scale in `styles.scss`** (`--app-btn-*`: the text of a button is `--app-fs-small`, the main button's `--app-fs-text`; the room round the text and the gap to the icon are in `em`, so they follow the text size and a button keeps its shape; more room on a touch screen; the icon in a button is 1.15em and spaced by the gap, so a template needs no `me-1` on it): `.btn` the usual, `.btn-sm` the small one in rows and lists (also `.btn-group-sm > .btn`; it differs from the usual one by less room, not by its text), `.btn-main` (same as `.btn-lg`) the big one for the main action of a page. Never size a button in a template or a component.
- **Texts are translated (Transloco) in en, nb, sv, da, fi, de, fr, es, nl** (same way as the Seven Friends project): files `frontend/public/i18n/<lang>/<scope>.json`, scopes `play`, `userLevels`, `logons`, `install`, `guide`, `jigsawHome`, `jigsawList`, `jigsawUser`, `jigsawNew`, `jigsawPage`, `jigsawPlay`, `jigsawShell`, `jigsawBoard`, `jigsawBatch`, `jigsawCategories`, `jigsawCropper`, `jigsawExample`, `jigsawLevels` (the names of the `jigsaw...` scopes come from older pages; each holds texts of the components that use it now). A template declares `provideTranslocoScope('<scope>')` and `*transloco="let t"` and ALWAYS writes the English next to the key (`{{ t('jigsawHome.title') || 'Karins jigsaw' }}`); TypeScript uses `tr('scope.key', 'English')` from `core/i18n.ts`. New text: add the key to all nine languages in the same change, then run `npm run check:i18n`. The language is chosen in the preferences, on the account page and in the bars of the admin and of signing in (for nobody also in the menu of the home page), and kept in `localStorage` (`jigsaw.language`); until a language is chosen the language of the browser is used when the app has it (`browserLanguage()` in `core/language.service.ts`; Norwegian `nb`, `nn` and `no` are `nb`; never kept on the device: only a choice is), else English; a language chosen is kept, and the account's language wins once an account plays. The guide has one file per language (`frontend/public/i18n/<lang>/jigsaw-guide.md`). Not translated: messages that come from the API or from the generated shared models. A long text must wrap inside the view, never flow outside it (check at 320 px in sv).
- **The buttons that end a form (Save, Store, Cancel, Close, Start) are centred** (`d-flex flex-wrap justify-content-center gap-2`), never at the right edge, where the floating menu of the app lies at first.
- **A long form is grouped in sections** (`.form-section` in `styles.scss`: an `h2` with a small icon and words over a thin rule, `first` for the one at the top; used for the choice of a new round, many at once, the Sign-ins of the account and the pictures of a package). One form: labels are plain `form-label` (not `small`), a field has `mb-3` and a row of fields `g-3 mb-3`; Edit, Delete and the actions that change the picture are `.link-action` in a `.toolbar`, and so are the actions of a row of rounds (Continue, Look, Delete, and the question before a delete: `.link-action`, `.danger` for the delete); Cancel and Save of a form are `.btn-outline-secondary` and `.btn-primary`. New headings are new keys in all nine languages.
- **The toolbar is one standard in `styles.scss`** (a row of plain words, no boxes, no padding at the sides, so every text starts where it looks to start and lines up with what is above and below it): `.toolbar` the row (one gap size), `.segmented` a switch of choices in it (radio `btn-check` + `btn` labels; the picked one is blue, bold, underlined), `.link-action` a text that does something (icon and words; `.danger` for a delete), `.toolbar-note` the tiny line under it ("3 jigsaws"), and `.link-inline` a word in a sentence that does something. Use them for every such row, now and on future pages; no `btn-link`, `px-0` or `p-0` tricks in a template.
- **Hiding in templates:** Bootstrap's `d-flex`, `d-none` and friends use `!important`, so the `hidden` attribute (or `[hidden]`) does NOT hide an element that has one of them: use `@if` (the buttons that start or delete a round were once "hidden" this way and still showed).
- **Words:** in the pages and the guide one game of a jigsaw is a **round** ("New round", "Delete round", "Your rounds"); in the code and the API it is a **play** (`PlaySpec`, `/plays`, `plays.service.ts`). The older notes here and in `api-and-code.md` that say "game" mean a round.
- **Account, not user:** in every text the person reads (the nine languages, the English fallbacks in the templates, the messages of the API and of the model) the one who plays is an **account** ("Choose another account", "Delete this account", "Show other accounts"); the **user name** stays the name of the handle ("User name"), and **login** is a Firebase sign-in. In the code, the API, the stored data and the guide files the class is still `JigsawUser` (`JigsawUsers`, `jigsaw-users`, `X-Jigsaw-User`): only the words people read changed. A person who plays is called a player, and "By player" in the list of rounds stays.
- **Plain words for the sign-in:** the Firebase side is a **sign-in** (Google, an emailed link or a password; "Sign in", "Sign out"), the player is an **account**, and an account that only some sign-ins may use is **locked** (one lock icon everywhere: the list of accounts and the rounds of a locked account). Short sentences, one idea each; a message says what is wrong and what to do next. An account with one sign-in only is told to add a second, so it cannot be lost (`userLogins.lockoutHint`). The code keeps the old words (`logon`, `protected`); only the texts changed.
- **Hard parts are kept short:** the less used choices of the signed-in box (a link code, another sign-in) are behind **More** (`app-my-logon-users`), and no text shows a message meant for the developer (the errors of Firebase are turned into "try another way").

## The standard look of free text
Names, authors, facts (descriptions too) and counts use the classes `.text-name`, `.text-author`, `.text-facts` and `.text-count` of `styles.scss`; their colours, weights and spacing are the `--app-text-*` tokens on `:root`, the one place to change them. No template sets a colour or weight of its own for such text.
