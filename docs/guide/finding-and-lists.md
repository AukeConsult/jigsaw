# Finding jigsaws and the lists

Part of the project guide (moved out of `CLAUDE.md`; text unchanged). Read this before changing this area.

- **Jigsaws are read a page at a time, and one by one.** A list asks for a page at a time (`limit`, `page`). **One jigsaw is read by its id** (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id`, `ApiCollection.get`, null for 404 or a jigsaw the person may not see; the store's `get` reads the document itself, so an old jigsaw is found: `list()` is only the newest 100): the page before the puzzle, a round, the form of a picture and the band of the rounds going (each open round's jigsaw once, `MyRounds`) do not read the whole list. Not yet paged: the rounds of a user over all jigsaws (`userPlaysRouter` asks every jigsaw of that list).
- **My pictures (`app-my-jigsaws`, the list "Your jigsaws") is read a page at a time too, with words, an order and who sees them chosen on the server** (a user can have up to 500 jigsaws): `GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-search/mine?text=&sort=&visibility=&limit=&page=` (`JigsawSearch.mine`, in `functions/src/jigsaw/jigsaw-search.ts`; 401 without `X-Jigsaw-User`; 400 for a visibility or a sort that is not allowed) gives the jigsaws of the user who asks, the private and the hidden ones too, never another user's, as `{items, total, hasMore, counts}` where `counts` is how many there are for All, Public, Private and Hidden with the words kept. The sorts are `new`, `old` and `name` (not by the number of pieces: most pictures are cut in each round). The list asks for 25, adds the next 25 with "Show more" ("Showing n of m"), starts again from the first page when the words (after 0.3 s), the order, who sees them or the user change, and keeps the words, the order and the choice on the device (`jigsaw.userJigsawText`, `jigsaw.userSort`, `jigsaw.userVisibility`). The list has only what a list needs (no objects), so a change (who sees it, Recommended) first reads the whole jigsaw (`changed()`), since a save replaces the whole document. "Select all" ticks what is shown. **A press anywhere on a row or a card opens the page of the picture**, where it is changed; only the tick box, Recommended, Delete and the choice of who sees it do their own thing. **The short filter also has Recommended**, with how many. **A user has at most 5 pictures in the category Recommended** (`AccountLimits.recommendedError`, `RECOMMENDED_PICTURES_MAX`; publishers and admins too, counted by owner): the server refuses a sixth (403) when it is made or changed, the star says why, and the AI never chooses Recommended. A jigsaw is in at most three categories, and a press when they are full takes the place of the last one by itself (`JigsawCategories.pressed`). **The pictures are shown as rows or as cards**, chosen with two icons at the end of the row of the filter and kept on the device (`jigsaw.userView`, rows to begin with); a card has the same to do as a row (the box, Recommended and Delete lie on its picture), takes the colour of who sees it (`.own-card`) and follows the size of the tiles (`tile-grid`). **A card shows only the picture or also its info** (name, what it is, who sees it), chosen with two more icons that are there for cards (`jigsaw.userCardInfo`, with info to begin with); with only the picture the box, Recommended and Delete are still on it. **Every picture of the list, in a row or a card, has a tiny mark of who sees it** in its lower right corner (`.picture-mark`: the icons of the filter, a globe, a lock or a crossed-out eye; public is the globe here as everywhere in the app, so it is not taken for hidden).
- **Favorites:** a user can tick a picture with a heart on its tile in the app (`play-card`; none without a user who plays). A favorite is **one document for each user and jigsaw in its own collection `jigsaw-favorites`** (`models/shared/favorite.ts`: `JIGSAW_FAVORITES`, `JigsawFavorites.idOf(userId, jigsawId)` = `userId~jigsawId`, so ticking twice is the same document and taking away is deleting it; `userId`, `jigsawId`, `createdAt`). `functions/src/jigsaw/jigsaw-favorites.ts`: `GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-favorites` gives `{ids}` of the user who asks (the header `X-Jigsaw-User`; 401 without, 404 for a user that does not exist), `PUT /:jigsawId` ticks (404 for a jigsaw that is not there or that is private to someone else) and `DELETE /:jigsawId` takes away; a user's favorites are asked for by one equality on `userId` (no composite index); the favorites of a jigsaw that is deleted go with it. `FavoritesService` reads them once for each user who plays and changes a heart at once (put back when the server could not keep it). **My favorites** in the menu of the home page looks only at them: the search takes `favorites=true` (`JigsawQuery.favorites`, `JigsawFilter.favorites`), and the route keeps only the jigsaws in the favorites of the user who asks (none for nobody), for the list and for the counts.
- **Solved:** a tile shows a mark when the user who plays has solved the jigsaw (`SolvedService`, the ids of the jigsaws with a solved round of the user, read when the user is chosen and when the list opens), and a round that is solved is painted light green in the lists of rounds (`.round-solved`, `--app-solved-bg`). No page asks for only the solved ones now (and no page has a box for words to look for in the pictures of everybody); the search still takes `solved=true` (`JigsawQuery.solved`, `JigsawFilter.solved`), and the route keeps only the jigsaws the user who asks has a solved round on (`solvedBy` in `app.ts`: one question for each jigsaw that is shown, 25 at a time, none for nobody), for the list and for the counts.
- **Looking for jigsaws** (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-search`, `models/shared/jigsaw-search.ts`, `functions/src/jigsaw/jigsaw-search.ts`): by lists `categories` (categories and sub-categories, `nature`, `nature/forest`; a category is every jigsaw in it, up to 6), `languages` (codes of `JIGSAW_LANGUAGES`; a jigsaw without a language is English), `sizes` (in pieces: small up to 48, medium up to 150, large up to 352, huge above), `difficulties` (hard, medium, easy) and `owners` (user UIDs, up to 10), written with commas, and a free `text` (words in the name, the author or the description, any order and case, a part of a word is enough, at most 60 characters; the description is also sent with the answer, for the picture tiles to show (`TileDetails`); My pictures (`/mine`) does not send it), with `sort` (new, name, pieces), `limit` and `page`; the answer is `{items, total, hasMore}` of summaries (`JigsawSummary`: no `placed`). **Each list means "any of these", and the lists and the text together mean "and".** `GET /jigsaw-search/facets` (the same parameters) counts the jigsaws for each choice with the rest of the filter kept and that list left out, so a tick says what ticking one more gives (`JigsawSearch.facets`, `JigsawFacets`: the total, the categories and sub-categories, sizes, difficulties and languages that jigsaws have, and the owners by name; the filter of pictures, `/play/prefs`, asks it at each choice and shows what there is to tick: public or private, the categories and the languages). **How the jigsaws are read:** `store.load(owners, 500)` makes these Firestore queries **at the same time** (`Promise.all`), each with `select` of the summary fields (`SUMMARY_FIELDS`) and a limit of `SEARCH_MAX_READ` (500): the newest jigsaws overall (`orderBy createdAt desc`, which also gets the ones with no owner), and **up to 500 of each owner's** (`where ownerUid ==`, the owners are the users). The answers are put together (each jigsaw once, the newest first) and **everything else is done in memory on those summaries** (what matches, the counts, the order, the pages), so a search and the counts are over the same jigsaws and agree. What was read is kept for 10 seconds (and forgotten when a jigsaw is made, changed or deleted), since the filter asks at every tick. **Never an equality together with an order, a `!=` or a range in one query**: it needs a composite index, and the indexes of this shared project are not ours to deploy (the emulator does not check for missing indexes, so keep to this rule); for that reason an owner's jigsaws are asked for without an order, so an owner with more than 500 jigsaws has 500 of them read, which ones is not said. The jigsaws everybody sees and the private ones the person may see (`canSee`; only when the header says who asks) are looked at in memory too. Nothing is kept in the jigsaw documents for finding.
- **A picture entry in the search:** it is cut in each round, so it is found by **every size and every difficulty** (`JigsawIndex.sizeClassesOf` and `levelsOf`: all of them for a picture, one for a jigsaw) and counts in every size and difficulty of the counts; `kind` is a summary field (`SUMMARY_FIELDS`). In the order by pieces (fewest or most first) pictures come after the jigsaws (`JigsawSearch.byPieces`). There is no filter for the kind. Popular jigsaws count the rounds on the entry at every size. The Rounds list needs nothing new: a round says its own `total`.
- **Popular jigsaws** (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-popular`, up to ten, `POPULAR_SHOWN`; **no page shows them now**, `JigsawService.popular` is not called): `Popularity` (`models/shared/popularity.ts`) is the rule: a jigsaw's score is the rounds played on it (a round nobody has moved a piece in is not counted) plus a bonus for being new, 5 on the day it is made and halved every 14 days, so a new jigsaw nobody has played yet can still be seen; the same score puts the newer jigsaw first. Only the jigsaws the person may see are ranked (the same `canSee` as the list).
