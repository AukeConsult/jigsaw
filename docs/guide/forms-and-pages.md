# Forms and pages

Part of the project guide. Read this before changing this area. The pages of the app are explained in `play-app.md`.

- **The first choice of a form is what is made** (the form of a picture, `play-picture-form`, and many at once, `app-jigsaw-batch`): **a picture** (to begin with, remembered as `kind` in `play.newPicture` and `jigsaw.batch`) or **a ready jigsaw**. For a picture there are no pieces, difficulty or clock; the picture is kept whole at `JigsawKinds.pictureSize` (or cut to a standard shape chosen in the form) and saving writes the document and the picture only. A ready jigsaw is cut to the shape chosen (`CutChoice`: size, pieces, difficulty, clock; the part of the picture that is used is found with the cropper) and its pieces are saved. What a saved picture is made as is not changed in the app afterwards (the server would still change the kind of one nobody has played). Where a ready jigsaw shows its pieces and difficulty, a picture shows a "Choose pieces" mark or leaves them out.
- **One form, two pages** (`/play/mine/addpicture` and `/play/mine/picture/:id`; `play/components/picture-form/`, its logic `NewPicture`). In the card (`app-jigsaw-header`): the picture, with a small icon in its upper right corner that adjusts the cut (**the cropper never opens by itself**: a picture gets the cut that covers its shape, `PictureCrop.covering`, round the middle of the cut it had), under it the size (**one size and cut for a picture and a ready jigsaw**, "The whole picture" for both; changing what is made keeps them); at the top what is made; then the name and the description (edited in place), "Advanced", the pieces, difficulty and clock of a ready jigsaw, who sees it, and Cancel and Save. The owner is the account that makes it (there is no choice of another), and there is no choice of the line and the colours. A new picture is private to begin with.
- **Easy to start (made for an average adult):** only what most people need is open; **"Advanced"** (`<details class="more-options">`: the categories and the motive difficulty) is closed to begin with and its state is kept on the device (`play.addPictureAdvanced`). A card always tells the picture and the name (unless the tiles show only pictures), and for a ready jigsaw the number of pieces and the difficulty; a picture says nothing of them on a card.
- **Saving runs the AI** (`ai-classification.md`). A new picture is not turned away when only the name is missing: the AI fills what is empty after it is saved; when the AI cannot, the name of the picture file (else "Picture") stands in. A saved picture that is changed needs a name for Save; the AI looks at it only when its picture file was replaced.
- **A picture that has been played** keeps its picture, size and cut. Its owner (or an admin) can take **all the rounds** away, also other players' (**Delete all rounds to change them**, asked first, `DELETE /jigsaw/:id/plays`), and then change everything; a new size or number of pieces cuts the saved picture again. Save and Cancel of a saved picture, and Delete on top of its page, go back to where it was opened from (My pictures, or the package).
- **Many at once** (`app-jigsaw-batch`, `app/jigsaw/jigsaw-batch/`; framed by `/play/mine/many`, for a publisher or an admin, and by the page of a package's pictures, where only pictures are made, each linked to the package): the person chooses pictures or ready jigsaws (pictures to begin with), for ready jigsaws the number of pieces (24 to 600), the width of the picture (600 to 1200 px), the difficulty and whether it is timed, and the language of the names and descriptions (the language the app is shown in to begin with); then **many pictures at once from the file system** (`<input type="file" multiple>`, jpeg, png or webp, at most `BATCH_MAX` = 30). They are made one after the other: `JigsawMaker` (`jigsaw-maker.ts`) saves each (owner: the user who plays, author: their name; private, except a picture of a package), uploads the picture, cuts a ready jigsaw and saves its pieces, and takes it away again when a step fails; then `JigsawService.classify(id, true)` puts the AI's categories, name and description on it (the pictures are sent to OpenAI, and the page says so). Each picture shows its state (waiting, cutting, saving, the AI looking, done or failed with why), a failed picture does not stop the others, the AI failing leaves what was made (it says why), and Stop ends after the one being made. The choices (not the pictures) are kept on the device (`jigsaw.batch`).
- **Deleting many of your pictures at once** (My pictures, `app-my-jigsaws`): each row has a checkbox, "Select all" ticks or clears them all (half-ticked when only some are), and "Delete the selected" asks first ("Yes, delete n" / Cancel) and then deletes them at the same time (`Promise.allSettled` over `JigsawService.removeAs`, so only the owner's succeed); the ones that could not be deleted stay ticked and the page says how many. The checkbox is lifted (`z-2`) because the row's `stretched-link` overlay would otherwise cover it and swallow the click. The ticks are for the moment only (not kept).
- **Share** (`jigsaw/share-button/`, `app-share-button`; `ShareLinks` in `jigsaw/share-link.ts`): on the page before the puzzle and the page of a picture (`play-share-picture`, which shares the address of the picture, with its key for a private one, `jigsaws-and-sharing.md`) and on the install document (which shares the page it is on). It opens the share dialog each system has (`navigator.share`: Android, iPhone and iPad, Mac, and Windows with Edge or Chrome); only a browser without it (for example Firefox on a computer) gets a short list: copy the link, Facebook, X, WhatsApp, e-mail (plain links that open the service with the page ready; nothing is sent until the person presses one).
