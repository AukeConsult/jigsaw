# The play module (`/play`): the app

Part of the project guide. Read this before changing anything in `frontend/src/play/`. The short version is in `CLAUDE.md` ("The app and its routes"); this file explains the module as a whole.

## What it is
`/play` is **the app** (`frontend/src/play/`): everything a player does is there. Beside it the site has only the admin (`/jigsaw`, `frontend/src/app/admin/`) and signing in (`/login`, `frontend/src/app/logons/`); the shared components and services are in `frontend/src/app/`. Home, `/`, and every address that is not known go to `/play/list`, and `/install` goes to `/play/info/install`. **The native wrappers (Android, iOS, Windows) always open `https://karins-jigsaw.web.app/play`** (`server.url` in `frontend/capacitor.config.json`, `AppUrl` in `client-wrappers/windows/MainWindow.xaml.cs`; change them together).

What a person does in it: look at the pictures, press one to put it together (a **round**), add pictures of their own, look at their pictures and rounds, change their account, and read the info about the app.

## The rules of the module
1. **The pages are the app's own, made of shared components.** The pages in `play/pages/` frame components of `frontend/src/app/` (`app-puzzel-board`, `app-my-jigsaws`, `app-jigsaw-header`, `app-welcome`, `app-guide-document` and so on). What two pages need is a component, framed by both; their links lead into `/play/...`.
2. **One component, framed into different pages**, so the person sees the same layout each time: the form of a picture (`play-picture-form`) is framed by Add picture and by the page of a picture.
3. **Every route carries a page and the menu that goes with it** (`data.menu`, a function of who plays, in `helper/menus.ts`, read by `play-route.ts`).
4. **The look is `styles.scss` only**: the five text sizes (`--app-fs-*`), the button sizes, the colour tokens, the page width `--app-page-max` (75 rem). Nothing sets a size or a font of its own (see `look-and-texts.md`). Every page is at most `.page-max` wide, except the puzzle, which fills the screen.
5. **Texts** are the scope `play` (`frontend/public/i18n/<language>/play.json`), in all nine languages like the rest of the app (`npm run check:i18n`); reused scopes (`jigsawNew`, `jigsawList`, `guide` ...) are translated too. The language is the account's, else the one chosen on the device, else the browser's if the app has it, else English; it is chosen in the preferences (and, for nobody, in the last row of the home page menu). The texts of the tiles of the home page come from the backend and are English for now.
6. **Every choice the person makes is kept on the device** (`readPreference`/`writePreference`, rule 12 of `CLAUDE.md`): the list filter, the switches of the menu, whether the menu is open, where it was put.

## The frame: main page and helper
- **`PlayMain`** (`play-main/`) is the main route. It fills the screen and is **the one thing that scrolls** (the window itself never does). It has one `<router-outlet>` (the page that is open), the admin band (`app-admin-banner`, when an admin is in another account) and the frame. It has the soft puzzle-piece pattern behind the pages (not behind the puzzle).
- **`PlayFrame`** (`play-frame/`) lies fixed over the page and everything on it and takes no clicks itself. It holds the helper. (There is no button of its own for the info pages: Help, the last entry of every menu, goes there.)
- **The helper** (`helper/helper.ts`) is **one movable menu on the main level**. It shows the menu of the open page: a list of entries, each an icon, words and the page a press goes to, or a **switch** (`toggle`) of the page that is open. A press is plain routing (`routerLink`): there are no helper views and no helper subclasses.
  - **The first entry of every menu is the name of the app** (the logo with the little house: it goes home, `/`). **There is no folding or collapsing** (no arrows): a menu always shows its entries. **That it can be moved is shown without words** (no hint text anywhere): a grip, a short thick line (on the top of the floating menu, on the edge of a bar that faces the page), which grows and takes colour a few times until the menu has been moved once (`play.helperMoved`; not when the device asks for less motion), and while it is carried the edges of the window light up where it can be fastened, with the one it will stick to filled. **A menu is kept small**, six entries or so besides the name of the app: what is chosen once is a preference. The first row is the same in every menu, sized by one parent class (`.play-helper .brand-item`).
  - It is dragged with the pointer, kept inside the window, grows from the side nearest to the edge when it opens or its words change, and its place is kept on the device. Until it is moved it is in the bottom right corner.
  - **A drag must stay cheap** (a tablet ran out of memory and froze): it is followed in `helper/menu-drag.ts` outside Angular, the menu is moved with a transform once for each frame, and **nothing else changes while it is carried**: it sticks, and the page makes room, when it is let go. One pointer moves it (a second finger or a palm does nothing); a press is a drag after 4 px with a mouse and 10 px with a finger; a click is not heard just after a drag; a long press opens no preview of a link (`-webkit-touch-callout`). Nothing large and blurred is drawn while it is carried.
  - **Dragged to an edge of the window it sticks there as a bar** (top, bottom, left or right; when it is let go with the pointer near the edge, 2.5 rem with a mouse and 5 rem with a finger, having come toward it, or with the menu pushed 2 rem past the edge; `.play-bar`, the second form in `helper/helper.html`). Across the top or bottom the entries are side by side on one line (**each entry is its icon with its word under it**, the room shared by how long the words are, a long text on two lines when there is little room, a word never broken; on a narrow screen the name beside the logo is left out); down a side they are one under the other, and on a narrow screen only the icons. The language is a button whose list opens over the page, away from the edge. **The page makes room for the bar** (`--app-play-bar-top`, `-bottom`, `-left`, `-right`, set by the helper; it also tells the window it was resized, so the board of a puzzle measures its room again, and `footerTop()` knows a bar at the bottom). **No text on a bar says how it is used:** its grip shows that it can be moved (above), and the logo with the little house goes home. **A bar flows together with the page**: the same soft pattern (`background-attachment: fixed` on both, so there is no seam), no line and no shadow; on the puzzle, which has no pattern, the bar is plain too. A bar stays on its edge until the pointer is dragged away from it (its thickness and 2 rem); dragged away it is the floating menu again, and the page takes the room back when it is let go. The edge is kept on the device.
  - **The language** is in the preferences, a pill with a flag for each language (for nobody it is also the last row of the home page menu before Help, `app-language-select`). The choice is kept on the device and as the account's language. Until a language is chosen the language of the browser is used when the app has it.
  - Switches that have nothing to switch are not offered (My favorites without favorites). A finished puzzle has no help picture and no border pieces to switch.
  - **A recommended picture** (the category Recommended) has a yellow star in the top left corner of its card.
- **The Filter entry has a dot while any choice is set** (`JigsawFilters.isOn`; also when both Public and Private are ticked, which lists everything).
- **The last entry of every menu is Help** (to `/play/info`; the info menu, with the other info pages, is there instead). **The way back from there is said in words** (a person did not understand how to get back): **Back** is the first entry of the info menu and a button that stays at the top of every info page; both go to `/play/info/back`, which leads to the page the person was on before the info, however many info pages were opened (`pageBeforeInfo` in `last-page.ts`, kept for the session so a reload keeps it; the home page when the visit began there). **Links between info pages take each other's place in the browser's history** (`replace` in `infoMenu`, `[replaceUrl]` on a link), so the device's own back button leaves the info in one press too: a new link from one info page to another does the same.
  - **A section opens where the person was last in it** (`last-page.ts`, kept with `core/preferences.ts` as `play.last.<section>`): Help (`/play/info`; the help to begin with), My stuff (`/play/mine`: My pictures or My rounds) and Publish (`/play/mine/publish`: many pictures, information, offers or packages). A new section of a menu is added there.

## The home page is a list of tiles
The list at `/play/list` shows **tiles**, not only pictures. `FeedItem` (`functions/src/models/shared/feed-item.ts`) is the parent class: `PictureItem` (made from a search result) and `KeptItem` with `InfoItem`, `OfferItem` and `PackageItem`. Pictures come from the search (`play-cards` asks it, with the filters and the endless scroll as before). The other tiles come from `GET /jigsaw-feed` (`FeedService`), which reads each kind's own collection and gives the tiles that are right and active now (`FeedItems.shown`): this is the one place where a rule of who is shown what will be added. `HomeFeed.merge` puts them among the pictures, by their place counted in pictures from the first one, so a page of pictures loaded later never moves a tile that is already there; a tile whose place is not loaded yet waits. The tiles that are not pictures are drawn by `app-feed-tile`.

**Information, offers and packages are written by a publisher or an admin** (`/play/mine/info`, `/play/mine/offers`, `/play/mine/packages`; a user menu entry each): a list with a search, a filter on the status (upcoming, active, expired) and a row for each tile with Edit and Delete, and a form to add or change one. A publisher has their own, an admin every tile (`FeedItems.mayChange`). Each kind has its own collection (`jigsaw-info`, `jigsaw-offers`, `jigsaw-packages`), its own routes (`/jigsaw-info`, `/jigsaw-offers`, `/jigsaw-packages`, `feedManageRouter`) and a date it is shown from and a date it expires. **The words are in every language**: the author writes in one language, the master language, and **the AI makes the other eight when the tile is saved** (`Translator`, `functions/src/jigsaw/translator.ts`; what it used is kept for the account that saved, `token-usage`, kind `translate`). An entry an author corrects by hand is never overwritten; the AI entries are made again when the master words change (`FeedItems.toTranslate`: the fingerprint of the master words, `sourceHash`). What is shown is the language that is chosen, else the master's, field by field. If the AI cannot be asked the tile is saved with its master words only and the page says so.

**Public, private or hidden.** Every tile says who sees it (the same choice as a jigsaw, `app-visibility-choice`): public (what none says), **private** (only its owner sees it on the home page, **with a clear banner on it, so it is not forgotten and left private**: it is for checking how it looks) or hidden (nobody sees it on the home page; it is only in its owner's list). **A new tile is private to begin with.** `GET /jigsaw-feed` takes who asks from the header (`FeedItems.shown(documents, now, viewer)`).

**A package** is like an offer (the words in every language, dates, visibility) and it **has a price or is free** (`price` in cents and `currency`; free is what none says), **is in categories** (the same as a picture's, at most three, chosen with `app-category-picker`; **the person's search on the home page shows a package only when it matches the chosen categories and the search words**, `FeedItem.matchesFilter`; information and offers are not content and are always shown) and **holds pictures**. The pictures are ordinary pictures in the same collection as all the others (`jigsaw`), each linked to the package by `packageId` (`JigsawSpec.packageId`; only the package's owner, or an admin, can put a picture in it); **they are not in the general list or the search** until a person has the package (below). The detail of a package (`/play/mine/packages/:id/pictures`) shows its pictures as cards, a press on one opens the page of the picture (`/play/mine/picture/:id?package=`; a link on top, Back to the package, Save, Cancel and Delete go back to the package, and a change never takes the picture out of it: the API keeps `packageId`), each can be deleted, and **many files are uploaded at once** (`app-jigsaw-batch` in its mode `packageId`: only pictures, each linked to the package). A package that has pictures cannot be deleted until they are (the list deletes them first, after asking). **A package on the home page is a tile with a random picture of its collection** (the answer names up to eight ids, chosen at random each time: `coverIds`, with `pictureCount`), the title, whether it is free or its price, and how many pictures it holds; pressing it opens the page of the package (`/play/package/:id`: its words, price and all its pictures).

Its owner (or an admin; they must still be a publisher) has **Edit the package** on top of that page, which opens its form (`/play/mine/packages/:id`).

**Only a verified account adds a package** (`users-and-logon.md`).

**Preferences** (`/play/account/preferences`, for everybody; Close at the bottom goes back, every choice is kept as it is made): the language and the home page (the rounds going, the size of the tiles and labels, the details on the tiles), the theme of the whole app and the sound of the puzzle (`pieces-and-play.md`), and the size and font of the text (`look-and-texts.md`), and **the size of the picture tiles** (`PartSizes`: small, medium, large, how many are in a row; kept on the device, `jigsaw.tileSize`, and with the account, `tileSize`; `play-main[data-tiles]`; a new grid of tiles takes the class `tile-grid`) and **the size of the small labels** the same way (every `.badge` of the app; `jigsaw.labelSize`, `labelSize`, `play-main[data-labels]`), and **whether the picture tiles show their details** (the description, the categories, how hard the motive is, who made it and when, and the clock and who-sees-it marks; **the pieces and the difficulty, or Choose pieces, are not details**: they are always on the tile; the class `tile-details`; yes to begin with; `jigsaw.tileDetails`, `tileDetails`).

**Looking is open, playing is not:** a picture the page of a package shows (package on show to the person, picture neither private nor hidden) is read by its id by anybody (`mayLook` in `app.ts`), and a locked card opens it; it is not found in the search or the lists.

**No round without the package:** `POST /jigsaw/:id/plays` refuses (403) a round for a player who has not got the package and owns neither the picture nor the package (**the owner of a package plays its pictures to try them out**), whoever sends the request (an admin neither, also when inside another account); the page before the puzzle shows no way to start one.

**Having a package.** On top of the page of a package is **Add to my pictures** (a free one) or **Buy** (a priced one; **nothing is charged: payment is not built**, and the page says so). The package is then kept under the account, in the subcollection `jigsaw-users/<id>/bought-packages` (`BoughtPackages`, `bought-package.ts`: the price it had, and `paid: false` for a priced one), by `PUT /jigsaw-users/:id/bought-packages/:packageId` (only the account itself, or an admin; only a package that is active and that the account may see). **From then on the pictures of the package are in that account's search, and the account may open and play them**; nobody else finds, opens or plays them but their owner and an admin (`BoughtPackages.mayUse`, asked by the search, one jigsaw, the lists and the rounds). **My pictures lists the packages the account has** (My packages, tiles above the pictures, only while there are any; `GET /jigsaw-feed/mine`, also a package that is not on the home page any more), but not their pictures one by one (decided: the search is enough): its list holds only what the user made. **In the home list a picture of a package has a tag** in the bottom left corner of its card with the name of the package (`.package-tag`; just Package when the home page has that package no more), which leads to the package; the cards on the page of a package have none. Until a person has it, the page of the package shows its pictures with a lock: they can be looked at, not opened. What was got stays on its page for that person also when the package is not on the home page any more.

The pages of the packages (`/play/packages` and `/play/package/:id`) have a menu of their own, `packagesMenu`: the way home, My stuff, Packages and Help (nobody: home, Packages, Logon and Help).

**Packages** (`/play/packages`, in the page menu, also for nobody, and linked from the top of each package) shows every package there is to get as tiles; the ones the account has are marked In my pictures. **The home page does not list a package the account has got** (`GET /jigsaw-feed` says `had`, and `HomeFeed.merge` leaves out what is not `onHome`): its pictures are in the list, each with the tag that leads to it, and All packages has it too.

## The puzzle page
The puzzle page does not scroll: it is as big as the screen. The details and **Table and sound** are panels over the puzzle, in its upper right corner, opened and closed from the menu (and their close button); both begin closed, and on a small screen a click outside them closes them. The working area reaches the top of the screen and the picture lies in from its top and left edge by `areaMargin` (a twentieth of the shorter side of the picture area, at least 20 px). A finished puzzle has the band on top with the time. The rules of the board are in `board-layout.md`, `rules-of-a-round.md` and `pieces-and-play.md`.

## Testing and running
There are no unit tests for the frontend: check pages in the browser (`npm run dev`, the frontend on http://localhost:4210/play). The model and the API have the real tests (`npm test`). After a change run `npx tsc -p tsconfig.app.json --noEmit --noUnusedLocals` in `frontend/`.


**Tiles:** every grid of tiles (home, packages, a package, its pictures, My pictures) has the same classes (`row tile-grid row-cols-2 row-cols-sm-3 row-cols-lg-4 row-cols-xxl-5`) and the pages use the whole width, so the tile size of the preferences gives the same tiles everywhere.
