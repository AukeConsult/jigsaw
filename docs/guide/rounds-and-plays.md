# Rounds (plays) and users' games

Part of the project guide (moved out of `CLAUDE.md`; text unchanged). Read this before changing this area.

- **A jigsaw is not played twice by the same user:** once a round of theirs is solved, `POST /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays` answers 409 for them (others may still play it), `POST .../plays/:playId/restart` answers 409 for a solved round, the page before the puzzle offers Look instead of Start for a solved ready jigsaw (and does not offer the sizes solved of a picture) and a solved round has no Reset round button; deleting the solved round is the way to play it again. "Start my own round" (looking at another player's round, in the Details panel) is `PuzzelRound.startOwn`.
- **A round of a picture has a cut and a difficulty of its own** (`PlaySpec.cut` = `{columns, rows, grid}` and `PlaySpec.snapPercent`, both optional; `PLAY_PIECES` = the pieces of that cut as documents `jigsaw/<id>/plays/<play id>/pieces/<piece id>`, the same documents as a jigsaw's pieces: the board draws the pieces from the picture). **The rule: the round's own value if it has one, else the jigsaw's, decided when the data is fetched**: `Plays.jigsawFor(jigsaw, play)` gives the jigsaw as the round plays it (the picture area `columns * grid` × `rows * grid`, the grid, the store and the difficulty of the round) and is the same object for a round with none of its own; the pieces come from `PlayStore.roundPieces` when the round has a `cut`, else from the jigsaw's pieces as always (the routes in `jigsaw/plays.ts` do this through `piecesOf`, the round already read by `ownRound`, so a normal round reads nothing more). **A normal round (every round of a ready jigsaw, every older round) is exactly as it was.** `POST /jigsaw/:id/plays` takes `{userId, columns, rows, snapPercent}` for a picture (`Plays.choiceError`: one of `JigsawCut.pieceChoices(width, height)` and one of the three difficulties; 400 otherwise, and 400 for a ready jigsaw that is given a choice); `Plays.startOwn` cuts on the server (`JigsawCut.cut` over `JigsawCut.layoutOf`) and the round and its pieces are stored by `PlayStore.create(…, pieces)` (the pieces first, then the round, so a round is never without them). `GET /:playId` adds `layout` (width, height, gridSize) only for such a round. Restart keeps the cut, the difficulty and the pieces. **Not played twice, per size:** a user cannot start a picture at a number of pieces they solved (`Plays.blockedBy`, 409), but can at another; only one unfinished round per user and picture (`Plays.goingError`, 409); a round only opened is returned when the choice is the same and replaced when it is not. Size and difficulty cannot change after the start (delete the round and start again). The page asks with `app-round-choice` (`jigsaw/round-choice/`: the numbers of pieces for the shape of the picture, the sizes solved left out, the difficulty; what was chosen is kept as `jigsaw.roundDefaults`, `Plays.choiceNear` picks the number nearest to it; the clock is chosen there too), "Start my own round" of a picture goes to the page before the puzzle (`/play/round/:id`), and `PuzzelRound` reads `layout` into `item`.
- **Looking at other players' rounds:** the page before the puzzle (`/play/round/:id`) and the page of a picture list the rounds (`play-picture-rounds`, rows of `app-round-row`; `GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays`), the user's own first and marked "Yours"; another player's round opens read-only (`PuzzelRound.viewOnly`: `JigsawBoard.viewOnly` stops moving, picking and collecting; the cheat switch, who sees the round, Reset round and Delete round are not rendered in the Details (`@if (!round.viewOnly())`), a button starts the user's own round). The API refuses to change or delete a round unless the header `X-Jigsaw-User` is its player's UID (403; `PlaysService` sends it); for a user with no verified Firebase login there is no sign-in, so this is not a lock (a user with verified logins is protected by the gate: see `users-and-logon.md`).
- **A jigsaw can be timed** (`timed` on the jigsaw: absent means timed, `false` means no clock; a switch for a ready jigsaw in the form of a picture and in many at once, and chosen for each round of a picture in `app-round-choice`). In a timed jigsaw the round has a clock (`elapsedMs` on the play, saved with `PATCH .../positions`; it only grows; `PuzzelRound`): it counts while the round is open on the screen, is the user's own and is not solved; it shows in the Details panel of the puzzle (`app-puzzel-details`), in the note on the picture of a finished round (it fades away) and in the lists of rounds. Another player looking at a round never counts.
- **Plays** (`functions/src/models/shared/play.ts`, `functions/src/plays.ts`, `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays`): a jigsaw is fixed (picture, size, grid, the cut into pieces, and the defaults of difficulty and look: its `snapPercent`, `lineWidth`, `lineColor`, `backColor`); a play is one game of it by one user, identified by the user's **UID** (`userId`; never the user name, which can change) with a copy of the user's name (`userName`, put there by the server when the game starts) (`jigsaw/<id>/plays/<play id>`, positions in the one document `state/positions` as a JSON text). **A play chooses nothing about how it looks or how hard it is** (the difficulty is the jigsaw's own `snapPercent`, the same in every round, and a round follows it when the jigsaw is edited; it is shown, not chosen, in the round; the look, the line and the colours, will be the account's, see `TODO.md`). A play document can still hold `overrides` of the look (`PLAY_OVERRIDE_KEYS`, only those three keys, kept by `Plays.publicOf`) from the time a round could choose them: nothing writes them any more (the route `PATCH /:playId/settings` is gone), but `Plays.effective` reads them, so such a round keeps its look; every other round follows the jigsaw. The difficulty is copied into the play when it starts. A jigsaw that has plays keeps its width, height, grid, difficulty, clock (timed), picture and cut (409). **Users** (`models/shared/jigsaw-user.ts`, `functions/src/jigsaw-users.ts`, `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users`): a person picks their user from the list on the account page, `/play/account` (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users`: id, user name and name by name, never the e-mail) or makes a user (a user name, 3-30 of `a-z0-9_-`, and a name); a user is a document in the collection `jigsaw-users` with a **UID** the database makes (the `id`: what a play's `userId` is), and the user name is a unique extra field used to look a user up; an optional private `email` (small letters, kept for a later connection to a Firebase registration, returned only by the POST that makes the user and never by a lookup: `JigsawUsers.publicOf`) can be given when the user is made and changed or taken away with `PUT /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users/:id` (`{name?, username?, email?}`, only what is sent changes, the user name is checked for being taken in a transaction, the UID and so the plays never change; the edit form on the account page changes the user name and the name, it has no e-mail field) (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users/by-username/:username`; made in a transaction so it is taken only once; a user an earlier version made has its user name as the document id and is read with it as the user name); the browser keeps the UID of the user who plays in `localStorage` (`jigsaw.userId`, `JigsawUserService`; the old key `jigsaw.username` held the id then, and is still read); a play is started only for a user that exists (`PlayDeps.userExists`, by UID). **Frontend:** `/play/round/:id` comes before a round (the choice for a picture; a look at a ready jigsaw), `/play/jigsaw/:id` plays one (`?round=` a given one, else `PuzzelRound.openFor`: the one going, else the solved one, else a new one; it reads the round with `PlaysService` and saves positions through `PieceSync` with the play id); Reset round and Delete round (asked first) are in the Details panel, and Delete goes home. **The games of a user** over all the jigsaws are listed on `/play/mine/rounds` (`app-my-round-list`; `GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users/:id/plays`, `userPlaysRouter`; one collection group question, see `api-and-code.md`). **Owner:** a jigsaw has an optional `ownerUid` (the UID of its author: the form gives a new one the account that plays, and a changed one keeps its owner); the API checks the user exists and puts the user's name in `author` itself (`stampOwner` in `app.ts`); every page that PUTs a jigsaw sends `ownerUid` back (a PUT replaces the document). **Editing:** `/play/mine/picture/:id` is the form of a picture filled in (`NewPicture.load`); `GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays/summary` says whether anybody has played it. Saving changes the settings, and when the picture, the cut or the size changed it uploads the picture and cuts the pieces again (`saveEdit`). Once it has been played only the name, the description, the language, the categories, the motive difficulty and who sees it can be changed (the server answers 409 to a change of size, grid, difficulty or clock), until the owner takes all the rounds away. Not done yet: **invites** (challenge a friend on a timed jigsaw: to be planned together, first reading how the Seven Friends project does its invites and using the same methods; nothing is built yet, and the help does not mention it), real sign-in and ownership, and the migration of existing jigsaws into plays (an old jigsaw is played through a new game, whose pieces are scattered anew).
- **"Reset round" (it was "New round") starts the round that is open over** (it does not make another one): `POST /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays/:playId/restart` (`Plays.restart`: the same round and player with its choices of look, the pieces scattered anew, no time played, start time now; it is a fresh round again until a piece is moved; only its player, 403 to others) and the page reads the round again (`PuzzelRound.startOver`, asked first, in the Details panel).

**A round started by a login is protected by its account.** When the call that starts a round carries a good token and its login is one of the verified logins of the jigsaw account that plays (`Plays.marked`, `logons.identify` puts the UID of the token in `res.locals.logonUid`), the round keeps that login's UID in `PlaySpec.logonUid` for later use. It is server only: every answer goes through `Plays.publicOf`, which takes it away and says `protected: true` instead, and the rows of rounds show a small lock for it (`app-round-row` for another player's round, `app-my-round-list`). A round of an open account, or started with no login, has none, and a round that was already started is not marked afterwards.

- **A round is public or private** (`PlaySpec.private`, kept only when true; `Plays.privateError`, `withPrivacy`, `mayLookAt`). It is chosen when a round is started (the pills "Who sees the round" in `app-round-choice`, remembered as part of `jigsaw.roundDefaults`; a ready jigsaw starts public and the choice is made in the Details panel) and changed later by its player in the Details panel of the round (`PATCH /jigsaw/:id/plays/:playId/privacy {private}`, the player only). **A private round is seen by its player only**: not among the rounds of its jigsaw (`GET /jigsaw/:id/plays`), not by its address (404), not among the games of the user for another person; an admin may look at it. Tests: `round-privacy.test.ts`.
- **How the places of a round are written** (`PlaySpec.places`, `PLAY_PLACES` = 2; see "Where a piece is saved" in `board-layout.md`): a round saved before that has no mark and is rewritten by `Plays.currentPlaces` when it is read (the answer only) and when a piece is saved (all of it, first, so old and new places are never mixed). Groups are moved whole.
