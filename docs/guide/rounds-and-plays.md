# Rounds (plays) and users' games

Part of the project guide (moved out of `CLAUDE.md`; text unchanged). Read this before changing this area.

- **A jigsaw is not played twice by the same user:** once a round of theirs is solved, `POST /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays` answers 409 for them (others may still play it), `POST .../plays/:playId/restart` answers 409 for a solved round, the page of the jigsaw shows "You have solved this jigsaw" instead of the Start button (`JigsawPage.solvedByMe`) and a solved round has no Reset round button; deleting the solved round is the way to play it again. "Start my own round" (looking at another player's round) is `JigsawPlay.startOwn`.
- **A round of a picture has a cut and a difficulty of its own** (`PlaySpec.cut` = `{columns, rows, grid}` and `PlaySpec.snapPercent`, both optional; `PLAY_PIECES` = the pieces of that cut as documents `jigsaw/<id>/plays/<play id>/pieces/<piece id>`, the same documents as a jigsaw's pieces, no graphics: the board draws the pieces from the picture). **The rule: the round's own value if it has one, else the jigsaw's, decided when the data is fetched**: `Plays.jigsawFor(jigsaw, play)` gives the jigsaw as the round plays it (the picture area `columns * grid` × `rows * grid`, the grid, the store and the difficulty of the round) and is the same object for a round with none of its own; the pieces come from `PlayStore.roundPieces` when the round has a `cut`, else from the jigsaw's pieces as always (the routes in `jigsaw/plays.ts` do this through `piecesOf`, the round already read by `ownRound`, so a normal round reads nothing more). **A normal round (every round of a ready jigsaw, every older round) is exactly as it was.** `POST /jigsaw/:id/plays` takes `{userId, columns, rows, snapPercent}` for a picture (`Plays.choiceError`: one of `JigsawCut.pieceChoices(width, height)` and one of the three difficulties; 400 otherwise, and 400 for a ready jigsaw that is given a choice); `Plays.startOwn` cuts on the server (`JigsawCut.cut` over `JigsawCut.layoutOf`) and the round and its pieces are stored by `PlayStore.create(…, pieces)` (the pieces first, then the round, so a round is never without them). `GET /:playId` adds `layout` (width, height, gridSize, storeWidth) only for such a round. Restart keeps the cut, the difficulty and the pieces. **Not played twice, per size:** a user cannot start a picture at a number of pieces they solved (`Plays.blockedBy`, 409), but can at another; only one unfinished round per user and picture (`Plays.goingError`, 409); a round only opened is returned when the choice is the same and replaced when it is not. Size and difficulty cannot change after the start (delete the round and start again). The page asks with `app-round-choice` (`jigsaw/round-choice/`: the numbers of pieces for the shape of the picture, the sizes solved left out, the difficulty; what was chosen is kept as `jigsaw.roundDefaults`, `Plays.choiceNear` picks the number nearest to it), "Start my own round" of a picture goes to the picture's page, and `JigsawPlay` reads `layout` into `item`.
- **Looking at other players' rounds:** the page of a jigsaw lists everyone's rounds (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays`), the user's own first and marked "Yours"; another player's round opens read-only (`JigsawPlay.viewOnly`: `JigsawBoard.viewOnly` stops moving, picking and collecting, the settings, the cheat switch and the New round and Delete round buttons are not rendered (`@if (!viewOnly())`), a button starts the user's own round). The API refuses to change or delete a round unless the header `X-Jigsaw-User` is its player's UID (403; `PlaysService` sends it); for a user with no verified Firebase login there is no sign-in, so this is not a lock (a user with verified logins is protected by the gate: see `users-and-logon.md`).
- **A jigsaw can be timed** (`timed` on the jigsaw: absent means timed, `false` means no clock; a switch in the form and in the edit panel of the jigsaw page). A timed jigsaw shows a "Timed" badge in the lists and on its page. In a timed jigsaw the round has a clock (`elapsedMs` on the play, saved with `PATCH .../positions`; it only grows): it counts while the round is open on the screen, is the user's own and is not solved; it shows in the menu on the left of the helper switches and in the lists of rounds. Another player looking at a round never counts.
- **Plays** (`functions/src/models/shared/play.ts`, `functions/src/plays.ts`, `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays`): a jigsaw is fixed (picture, size, grid, the cut into pieces, and the defaults of difficulty and look: its `snapPercent`, `lineWidth`, `lineColor`, `backColor`); a play is one game of it by one user, identified by the user's **UID** (`userId`; never the user name, which can change) with a copy of the user's name (`userName`, put there by the server when the game starts) (`jigsaw/<id>/plays/<play id>`, positions in the one document `state/positions` as a JSON text). **A play can only choose the look** (the line and the colours; the difficulty is the jigsaw's own `snapPercent`, the same in every round, and a round follows it when the jigsaw is edited; it is shown, not chosen, in the round) (`PLAY_OVERRIDE_KEYS`; `Plays.overridesError` refuses every other key, so size, picture and pieces can never be changed by a play). The difficulty is copied into the play when it starts; the look follows the jigsaw until the play chooses its own (`Plays.effective`). A jigsaw that has plays keeps its width, height, grid, difficulty, clock (timed), picture and cut (409). **Users** (`models/shared/jigsaw-user.ts`, `functions/src/jigsaw-users.ts`, `/jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users`): there is no sign-in; a person picks their user from the list on `/jigsaw/user` (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users`: id, user name and name by name, never the e-mail) or makes a user (a user name, 3-30 of `a-z0-9_-`, and a name); a user is a document in the collection `jigsaw-users` with a **UID** the database makes (the `id`: what a play's `userId` is), and the user name is a unique extra field used to look a user up; an optional private `email` (small letters, kept for a later connection to a Firebase registration, returned only by the POST that makes the user and never by a lookup: `JigsawUsers.publicOf`) can be given when the user is made and changed or taken away with `PUT /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users/:id` (`{name?, username?, email?}`, only what is sent changes, the user name is checked for being taken in a transaction, the UID and so the plays never change; the form on `/jigsaw/user` has Edit) (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users/by-username/:username`; made in a transaction so it is taken only once; a user an earlier version made has its user name as the document id and is read with it as the user name); the browser keeps the UID of the user who plays in `localStorage` (`jigsaw.userId`, `JigsawUserService`; the old key `jigsaw.username` held the id then, and is still read); a play is started only for a user that exists (`PlayDeps.userExists`, by UID). `userGuard` sends a person without a user to `/jigsaw/user?next=...` first. **Frontend:** `/jigsaw/:id` is the page of a jigsaw (`JigsawPage`: its picture and the user's games, Continue, New game, Delete, rename), `/jigsaw/:id/play/:playId` plays one game (`JigsawPlay` reads the game with `PlaysService`, saves positions through `PieceSync` with the play id, and saves the look choices as the play's `overrides` with `Autosave`; the page reloads itself when the play id in the route changes, so New game is a navigation); `Start over` is now New game, and Delete game (asked first) deletes the open game and goes to the page of the jigsaw. **The games of a user** over all the jigsaws are listed on `/jigsaw/user` (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw-users/:id/plays`, `userPlaysRouter`: it asks every jigsaw for the user's plays, one equality each, so no Firestore index; a collection-group query on `plays` by `userId` would replace it, and needs a collection-group index entry that you deploy). **Owner:** a jigsaw has an optional `ownerUid` (the UID of its author, chosen from the users in a select on the form of a new jigsaw, which starts as the user who plays; the page for a jigsaw that was played has the same select); the API checks the user exists and puts the user's name in `author` itself (`stampOwner` in `app.ts`); every page that PUTs a jigsaw sends `ownerUid` back (a PUT replaces the document). **Editing:** `Edit` on the jigsaw page opens `/jigsaw/:id/edit`, the form of a new jigsaw filled in (`JigsawNew` with `editId`), while `GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays/summary` says nobody has played it; saving changes the settings, and when the picture, the cut or the size changed it uploads the picture and cuts the pieces again (`saveEdit`). Once it has been played, `Edit` opens the small panel on its page (name, description, author, who sees it and the look; the difficulty and the clock are shown and cannot be changed; the server answers 409 to a change of size, grid, difficulty or clock). Not done yet: **invites** (challenge a friend on a timed jigsaw: to be planned together, first reading how the Seven Friends project does its invites and using the same methods; nothing is built yet, and the help does not mention it), real sign-in and ownership, the migration of existing jigsaws into plays (an old jigsaw is played through a new game, whose pieces are scattered anew), the front page's "continue" row from the user's games, and the collection-group index for "my plays" across jigsaws.
- **"Reset round" (it was "New round") starts the round that is open over** (it does not make another one): `POST /jigsaw/api/jigsaw/:id/plays/:playId/restart` (`Plays.restart`: the same round and player with its choices of look, the pieces scattered anew, no time played, start time now; it is a fresh round again until a piece is moved; only its player, 403 to others) and the page reads the round again (`JigsawPlay.startOver`, asked first).

**A round started by a login is protected by its account.** When the call that starts a round carries a good token and its login is one of the verified logins of the jigsaw account that plays (`Plays.marked`, `logons.identify` puts the UID of the token in `res.locals.logonUid`), the round keeps that login's UID in `PlaySpec.logonUid` for later use. It is server only: every answer goes through `Plays.publicOf`, which takes it away and says `protected: true` instead, and the rows of rounds show a small shield for it (`round-row`, the rounds of the account page). A round of an open account, or started with no login, has none, and a round that was already started is not marked afterwards.
