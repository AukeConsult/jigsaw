# Jigsaws, who sees them, and their pictures

Part of the project guide (moved out of `CLAUDE.md`; text unchanged). Read this before changing this area.

- **A jigsaw** is a document with a name, a size in pixels (whole), a grid, a difficulty and a look, an owner and who sees it, and whether it is timed. The size, the picture, the pieces, the difficulty and whether it is timed are fixed once somebody has started a round (the service answers 409 to a change of them for everybody, the owner too, whoever has the address: `JigsawSettings.changesWhatPlayersShare` for the settings, and a guard on every route that puts in, takes away or moves the picture or the pieces; what can still change is the name, the description, the author, who sees it, and the look; the edit panel shows only those). **Who may change a jigsaw:** only its owner (the service answers 403 to anybody else for the settings, delete, and taking away or moving the picture and pieces; a jigsaw from before owners has no owner and is anybody's; the owner can hand it over by changing the author). `X-Jigsaw-User` says who asks, and for a user with no verified Firebase login this keeps the app honest, it is not a lock (a user with verified logins is used only by them: see `users-and-logon.md`). **A jigsaw only its owner sees** (`visibility: "owner"`) is listed for its owner and for the players who have a round on it (also one that is only opened); they keep their rounds and go on with them, the page says it is private, and only the owner starts another round (403 to a player, 404 to anybody else, also for its rounds); its picture and the graphics of its pieces are read only with its **key** (see "The pictures of a private or hidden jigsaw" below).
- **Public, private, hidden** (`JigsawSpec.visibility`: `everyone` (public, what none says), `owner` (private, above) or `hidden`; the rules are `JigsawVisibilities.restricted` (private and hidden: only the owner and the players with a round open it, 404 to others) and `.listed` (not hidden), used by the routes). **A hidden jigsaw is only in the list "Your jigsaws" of its owner** (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw`): never in the search, the front page, the popular ones, the counts, not even for its owner, and its page is opened by its address. Private and hidden need an owner. Chosen in the select "Who sees it" on the forms of a jigsaw, and for many at once on the user page: tick jigsaws and use the select "Who sees it…" beside "Delete the selected" (each ticked jigsaw is saved at the same time as it is, with the new visibility; `JigsawSettings(...).stored()`); a row shows a lock or an eye-slash when it is private or hidden. A welcome ("Welcome back, name") on the front page is shown only when the person was last active more than an hour ago (`core/activity.ts`, `Activity.awayLong`, worked out once when the app opens). The page of the user is wider (`.form-card.wide`). The button "Enter a round, solve it" continues the user's newest unsolved round when there is one. Pictures cannot be dragged away (`img { -webkit-user-drag: none }`).
- **The pictures of a private or hidden jigsaw** (`functions/src/jigsaw/view-key.ts`, `image-access.ts`; the model field is `JigsawSpec.imageKey`, **output only**). A picture is read with `<img src>`, which cannot say who asks, so **the answers that tell about a private or hidden jigsaw carry a key for its pictures, and the page puts it on the address** (`?k=`): `GET /jigsaw` and `/jigsaw/:id`, the saved jigsaw that `POST` and `PUT` answer with, the search and `/mine`, the popular ones, and the jigsaw of each round in `GET /jigsaw-users/:id/plays` (`UserGame.jigsaw.imageKey`; only to somebody who may see that jigsaw, the rounds listed can be another user's). A jigsaw everybody sees has none, and its pictures are read with no key, as before. The key is `<bucket>.<HMAC-SHA256(secret, jigsaw id + bucket)>` (22 characters of it), a bucket is 6 hours and the current and the previous are accepted, so it works for 6 to 12 hours and is the same for everybody given it (the address of a picture stays the same, so the browser keeps the picture); the secret is a random text in the document `jigsaw-config/view-key` made the first time it is needed (delete the document to end every key; tests give their own with `viewKeySecret`). `GET`/`HEAD` of `/jigsaw-image/:id` and `/jigsaw-pieces/:id/graphic/:pieceId` go through `ImageAccess.guard` (before their routes, in `jigsaw/app.ts`): **a good key lets the request through without asking about the jigsaw** (a round reads hundreds of graphics), otherwise the visibility of the jigsaw is asked (kept 30 s, forgotten when a jigsaw is made, changed or deleted), a private or hidden one answers **404** (as for a picture that is not there), a question that cannot be answered answers 503 and never lets a picture through. Who is given the key is decided at that moment with the rules of the jigsaw (`canSee`: the owner, or a player with a round); it needs no login and no user that exists in the database, so it works for the users there are now and for nobody (a public jigsaw). **The key is never kept:** not in a jigsaw (`JigsawInput` has no `imageKey`, so `stored()` drops it when the page sends back what it was given), a round, a favorite, `localStorage` or a link; a stored reference to a picture is the jigsaw's id only (`jigsaw/<id>/picture`, `jigsaw/<id>/pieces/<piece id>`), so changing who owns a jigsaw or who a user is never leaves a picture unreadable or a key behind, and `functions/test/image-access.test.ts` checks that nothing stored holds one. **Frontend:** every picture address is made with `JigsawImageService.url(jigsaw, version)` (and `has(jigsaw)`, `JigsawPiecesService.graphicUrl(jigsaw, pieceId)`), which takes the jigsaw or summary that was read and adds `&k=` when it has an `imageKey`: never build an address from an id alone. For a user with no logon "private" and "hidden" are a way of not showing, not a lock (the server only sees an id in `X-Jigsaw-User`); it is a lock only for a user with verified logins (see `users-and-logon.md`). Writes are unchanged (the owner guards). Not yet covered: the pieces data route (`GET /jigsaw-pieces/:id`, places only, no picture) is still open, and a round left open for more than 12 hours does not refresh its key (it is read again when the page is opened).
