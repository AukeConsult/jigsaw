# Jigsaws, who sees them, and their pictures

Part of the project guide (moved out of `CLAUDE.md`; text unchanged). Read this before changing this area.

- **A jigsaw** is a document with a name, a size in pixels (whole), a grid, a difficulty and a look, an owner and who sees it, and whether it is timed. The size, the picture, the pieces, the difficulty and whether it is timed are fixed once somebody has started a round (the service answers 409 to a change of them for everybody, the owner too, whoever has the address: `JigsawSettings.changesWhatPlayersShare` for the settings, and a guard on every route that puts in, takes away or moves the picture or the pieces; what can still change is the name, the description, the author, who sees it, and the look; the form of a picture locks the rest). **Who may change a jigsaw:** only its owner (the service answers 403 to anybody else for the settings, delete, and taking away or moving the picture and pieces; a jigsaw from before owners has no owner and is anybody's; the API lets the owner hand it over by changing the `ownerUid`, the app has no choice of it). `X-Jigsaw-User` says who asks, and for a user with no verified Firebase login this keeps the app honest, it is not a lock (a user with verified logins is used only by them: see `users-and-logon.md`). **A jigsaw only its owner sees** (`visibility: "owner"`, private) is **found in the list of the home page, in the search and among the popular ones, and in the list `GET /jigsaw`, by its owner and by each player who has a round on it** (`canFind` in `app.ts`; a round that is only opened counts), and by nobody else: not by another account, not by a visitor, and **not by an admin either** (an admin finds only their own; an admin may open it by its address, see `users-and-logon.md`); its rounds are not in any list of rounds of others either; they keep their rounds and go on with them, the page says it is private, and only the owner starts another round (403 to a player, 404 to anybody else, also for its rounds); its picture is read only with its **key** (see "The pictures of a private or hidden jigsaw" below).
- **Two kinds of entry** (`JigsawSpec.kind`, `models/shared/jigsaw-kind.ts`, `JigsawKinds`): a **ready jigsaw** (what a document that says nothing is, so every older document: cut into pieces when it is made, with its grid, store and difficulty fixed in the document) and a **picture** (`kind: 'picture'`: only the stored picture with its name, description, categories, visibility, clock and look; the **pieces and the difficulty are chosen in each round**, see `rounds-and-plays.md`). A picture stores `width` and `height` as the size of its picture (`JigsawKinds.pictureSize`: 1200 wide to begin with, not wider than the picture, never more than `PICTURE_MAX_SIDE` = 2000 on a side), `gridSize` 0, `gridVisible` false and no objects; `stored()` drops a grid, a store and a snap range and `error()` refuses them (400). It has no pieces: `GET /jigsaw-pieces/:id` gives none and `PUT` answers 409 ("A picture is not cut: its rounds are"). The kind can change only while nobody has played it (`changesWhatPlayersShare` includes it, so 409 afterwards); making a ready jigsaw a picture takes its pieces away on the server, and making a picture a ready jigsaw is a new cut by the form. The motive difficulty and the AI classification work the same for both kinds (the classification keeps the kind).
- **Public, private, hidden** (`JigsawSpec.visibility`: `everyone` (public, what none says), `owner` (private, above) or `hidden`; the rules are `JigsawVisibilities.restricted` (private and hidden: only the owner and the players with a round open it, 404 to others) and `.listed` (not hidden), used by the routes). **A hidden jigsaw is only in the list "Your jigsaws" of its owner** (`GET /jigsaw/api/jigsaw`): never in the search, the list of the home page, the popular ones, the counts, not even for its owner, and it is opened by its address. Private and hidden need an owner. Chosen with the pills "Who sees it" on the form of a picture (`app-visibility-choice`), in a row of My pictures, and for many at once there: tick pictures and press a choice in the panel that opens (each ticked one is saved at the same time as it is, with the new visibility; `JigsawSettings(...).stored()`); a row has the soft colour of who sees it. Playing a jigsaw goes on with the user's unsolved round when there is one. Pictures cannot be dragged away (`img { -webkit-user-drag: none }`).
- **The pictures of a private or hidden jigsaw** (`functions/src/jigsaw/view-key.ts`, `image-access.ts`; the model field is `JigsawSpec.imageKey`, **output only**). A picture is read with `<img src>`, which cannot say who asks, so **the answers that tell about a private or hidden jigsaw carry a key for its pictures, and the page puts it on the address** (`?k=`): `GET /jigsaw` and `/jigsaw/:id`, the saved jigsaw that `POST` and `PUT` answer with, the search and `/mine`, the popular ones, and the jigsaw of each round in `GET /jigsaw-users/:id/plays` (`UserGame.jigsaw.imageKey`; only to somebody who may see that jigsaw, the rounds listed can be another user's). A jigsaw everybody sees has none, and its pictures are read with no key, as before. The key is `<bucket>.<HMAC-SHA256(secret, jigsaw id + bucket)>` (22 characters of it), a bucket is 6 hours and the current and the previous are accepted, so it works for 6 to 12 hours and is the same for everybody given it (the address of a picture stays the same, so the browser keeps the picture); the secret is a random text in the document `jigsaw-config/view-key` made the first time it is needed (delete the document to end every key; tests give their own with `viewKeySecret`). `GET`/`HEAD` of `/jigsaw-image/:id` go through `ImageAccess.guard` (before the route, in `jigsaw/app.ts`): **a good key lets the request through without asking about the jigsaw** (a list shows many pictures), otherwise the visibility of the jigsaw is asked (kept 30 s, forgotten when a jigsaw is made, changed or deleted), a private or hidden one answers **404** (as for a picture that is not there), a question that cannot be answered answers 503 and never lets a picture through. Who is given the key is decided at that moment with the rules of the jigsaw (`canSee`: the owner, or a player with a round); it needs no login and no user that exists in the database, so it works for the users there are now and for nobody (a public jigsaw). **The key is never kept:** not in a jigsaw (`JigsawInput` has no `imageKey`, so `stored()` drops it when the page sends back what it was given), a round, a favorite, `localStorage` or a link; a stored reference to a picture is the jigsaw's id only (`jigsaw/<id>/picture`, `jigsaw/<id>/pieces/<piece id>`), so changing who owns a jigsaw or who a user is never leaves a picture unreadable or a key behind, and `functions/test/image-access.test.ts` checks that nothing stored holds one. **Frontend:** every picture address is made with `JigsawImageService.url(jigsaw, version)` (and `has(jigsaw)`), which takes the jigsaw or summary that was read and adds `&k=` when it has an `imageKey`: never build an address from an id alone. For a user with no logon "private" and "hidden" are a way of not showing, not a lock (the server only sees an id in `X-Jigsaw-User`); it is a lock only for a user with verified logins (see `users-and-logon.md`). Writes are unchanged (the owner guards). Not yet covered: the pieces data route (`GET /jigsaw-pieces/:id`, places only, no picture) is still open, and a round left open for more than 12 hours does not refresh its key (it is read again when the page is opened).


## Who may do what with a jigsaw (the rules on one page)
A jigsaw is `everyone` (public), `owner` (private) or `hidden`. The server decides every one of these (`functions/src/jigsaw/app.ts`, `jigsaw-search.ts`, `plays.ts`, `image-access.ts`); the page only shows what the server allows.

| | public | private (`owner`) | hidden |
|---|---|---|---|
| **found on the home page, in the search, the popular ones** | everybody | its owner, and each player who has a round on it | nobody (not even its owner) |
| **in the list `GET /jigsaw`** | everybody | its owner, and each player who has a round on it | its owner |
| **in My pictures** | its owner | its owner | its owner |
| **opened by its address, its rounds, its picture** | everybody | its owner, a player who has a round on it, an admin, whoever has its link; 404 for others | its owner, a player who has a round on it, an admin; 404 for others |
| **a round started on it** | any account | its owner, an admin, and an account with its link | its owner and an admin |
| **changed, its picture or pieces changed, deleted** | its owner or an admin | its owner or an admin | its owner or an admin |

- **A link to a picture** (`functions/src/jigsaw/share-link.ts`; frontend `app/jigsaw/share-keys.ts`, `play/components/share-picture/`). The owner of a **private** picture sends a link, `/play/round/<id>#k=<key>`: **whoever gets it can look at that picture and play it**, also with no account for looking, and nothing else (no change, no delete, not found in any list). The key is an HMAC of the picture's id and a number (`jigsaw-shares/<id>`), never kept; **Make a new link** (on the picture's page, owner only) raises the number and stops every link sent before; somebody who has already started a round keeps it. The page keeps the key on the device (`jigsaw.shareKeys`), takes it out of the address and sends it as `X-Jigsaw-Share` with the calls about that picture. A round started through a link is private. A **hidden** picture and a **picture of a package** have no link (a link never stands in for getting or buying a package). A public picture is shared by its plain address. `GET`/`POST /jigsaw/:id/share`: the owner only (404 for others, an admin too).
- **Making a jigsaw public** is for a publisher or an admin (`UserLevels.publishError`); a user makes private or hidden ones; one that is public already stays public when its owner changes it.
- **Rounds:** a round is public or private (`PlaySpec.private`). A private round is not listed for others among the rounds of a picture: only its player (and an admin) sees it. A public round is listed under its picture for whoever may open the picture. The user's own band of rounds going shows their own rounds always.
- **The picture itself** of a private or hidden jigsaw is read with a key that the answers give only to those who may open the jigsaw (`image-access.ts`); the key is never kept.
- **Levels count only for a verified account** (one with a verified login): an account with none can only be a user, whatever is written on it.
- **A jigsaw changed after a round was started** keeps what every round shares (size, pieces, picture, difficulty, clock); this holds for the owner and an admin too.

## A picture of a package
A picture with a `packageId` belongs to a package of the home page (`play-app.md`). Whatever its visibility says, it is also only for its owner, an admin and **the accounts that have the package** (`jigsaw-users/<id>/bought-packages`, `BoughtPackages.mayUse`): the search, the lists, the popular ones, the page of one jigsaw and its rounds all ask that. Its image file is not locked: the package page shows it as a preview.
